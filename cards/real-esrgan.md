# Real-ESRGAN

Open-source image restoration model that fixes blurry, noisy, compressed, scratched, and black-and-white photos.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub/Hugging Face)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes — runs locally; total model size ~42 GB so needs a higher-end GPU for full quality
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-04-01

## What it does
Restores real-world degraded images by sharpening, removing noise, fixing compression artifacts, colorizing black-and-white photos, and removing effects like rain, snow, and reflections. Benchmarks show performance on par with closed models like GPT-4 Image and NanoBanana Pro, and ahead of other open-source options like Qwen Image Edit.

## When to use it
Restoring old or damaged photos, upscaling low-resolution images, or cleaning up compressed screenshots and thumbnails. Best open-source option currently for this specific class of image repair tasks.
