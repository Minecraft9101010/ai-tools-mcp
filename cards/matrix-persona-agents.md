# Matrix (Persona Agent Simulation)

Simulates 8.3 billion AI "persona agents" with distinct human characteristics to test products, surveys, or chatbots at population scale instead of recruiting real testers.

## What it does
Simulates persona agents (8.3 billion of them, with 1,290 persona attributes, across 1,000+ applications) that interact with apps, shopping sites, surveys, or chatbots to generate feedback, scores, and interaction data — an alternative to recruiting large numbers of real human testers for census-style surveys or user-journey testing. Open question acknowledged in the source material: how closely simulated persona behavior actually matches real humans.

## When to use it
Not yet clearly accessible; conceptually relevant for teams exploring simulated-user testing at scale once (if) it becomes available.
