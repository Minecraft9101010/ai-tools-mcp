# Unimate

An open-source AI model for animating rigged 3D characters and objects with unusual skeletons from text instructions.

- **URL:** https://linzhanmou.com/unimate/
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes, the transcript says the code, training code, and local setup instructions are released
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-13

## What it does
Unimate takes a rigged 3D model plus a text instruction and generates motion automatically. It is designed to handle non-human and unusual character skeletons without retraining.

## When to use it
Use it to animate non-standard 3D characters such as animals, creatures, objects, or stylized models.
