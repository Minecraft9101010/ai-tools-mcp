# Actionable World Representation

Builds controllable, moving 3D digital twins of real-world objects from point clouds or depth video, modeling how objects bend and articulate.

## What it does
Takes real-world 3D data (point clouds, or even just a depth video) and outputs a controllable 3D model that captures the object's motion, not just its appearance. Handles articulated objects like hands and full humans, deformables like earphones, and even the Unitree robot dog.

## When to use it
Robotics and embodied-AI work that needs accurate representations of how objects actually move, not just static meshes. Building simulation assets where articulation and deformation matter.
