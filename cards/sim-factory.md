# SimFactory

Turns one photo or video into a physically accurate, simulation-ready 3D scene that robots can train in, with automatic scene variations.

## What it does
Converts a real scene photo into a physics-accurate digital simulation for reinforcement-learning robot training, so a robot can fail thousands of times digitally instead of in a real kitchen or factory. Can generate variations (different objects, tables, doors, handles) so policies generalize instead of memorizing one setup.

## When to use it
Scaling robot training data from real environments, once (if) it ships; paper only for now.
