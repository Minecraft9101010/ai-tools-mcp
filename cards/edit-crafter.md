# EditCrafter

Image editing model that handles resolutions up to 4K (4096x4096), applying text-prompt edits while preserving high-resolution detail.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub code released)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (24 GB VRAM for 4K editing)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-04-26

## What it does
EditCrafter edits images at up to 4096x4096 resolution via text prompts. Examples: change cherry blossoms to maple, replace background, convert forest to burning forest. Preserves fine detail at high resolution. Caveat: tends to add contrast and saturation, so colors may not exactly match the original. Requires 24 GB VRAM for full 4K operation.

## When to use it
High-resolution image editing where maintaining original detail matters. Print-quality work (posters, photography) where 4K resolution is required. When you want open-source control over image editing at resolutions that most tools don't support.
