# Happy Oyster

Alibaba ATH Lab's open-ended 3D world model — a Genie 3 competitor that generates interactive real-time 3D environments from prompts.

## What it does
Generates open-ended 3D worlds that you can walk around and interact with in real time. Supports a wide variety of scenes and characters — riding a horse, paragliding, skateboarding, riding a dragon. You can prompt further to direct the scene. From the same ATH lab that built Happy Horse (top video generator).

## When to use it
Exploratory world-building, game prototyping, or interactive content creation once access opens up. Competitor to Google Genie 3.
