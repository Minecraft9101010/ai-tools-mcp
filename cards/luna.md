# Luna (Meta)

Animates realistic 3D human avatars from a few photos plus any driving signal (video, skeleton animation, even line art), skipping rigged skeletons entirely.

## What it does
Reconstructs and animates a 3D model of a target person from a few images, driven by a video of someone else moving, an animated skeleton, or a sketch animation. Converts every driving signal into Gaussian deformations instead of a pose skeleton, which is what makes it input-flexible and avoids the clothing-deformation and stiffness artifacts of skeleton-rigged pipelines.

## When to use it
Avatar animation research or watching for a future release; as a Meta paper with no code, nothing is usable yet.
