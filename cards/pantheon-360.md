## What it does
Takes several 360-degree images plus a camera path and outputs a high-quality panoramic video that stays spatially consistent as the camera moves — solving the drift/inconsistency problem regular (narrow-field-of-view) video generators have when stitched into a panorama. Reconstructs a rough 3D point cloud from the input images first, then uses that as grounding to keep the generated video stable along the path.

## When to use it
Building digital twins, or training data for robotics/autonomous driving, where the scene needs to stay geometrically consistent across a full camera path. Not yet released.
