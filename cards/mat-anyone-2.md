# Mat Anyone 2

Video matting model that separates people from backgrounds with high precision.

- **URL:** https://huggingface.co/ (search "Mat Anyone 2")
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local | web (HuggingFace Space)
- **Local-friendly:** yes — model is only ~140MB; free HuggingFace Space for online use
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-03-20

## What it does
Detects and segments any person from a video background with a single click, producing a clean alpha mask. Works on challenging cases including fast motion, complex hair, multiple characters, and stylized 3D characters. Outperforms GVM on edge quality.

## When to use it
VFX compositing, background removal in footage, rotoscoping prep. Works with both live-action and animated characters. Click on the person in the first frame and it tracks through the video automatically.
