## What it does
TTS model built on LTX 2.3 with voice cloning and director-style stage directions for controlling emotion and pacing. Supports accent changes, multi-language output, and inline emotion tags mid-transcript. Requires 16GB VRAM minimum and 32GB RAM.

## When to use it
Expressive voice acting for games, audiobooks, or video where flat TTS breaks immersion. Compare with Dramabox if phonetic vocalizations (laughter, sighs) or inline SFX are a priority.
