# Prism Audio

Open-source AI that takes a silent video and automatically generates realistic, synchronized sound effects matching the on-screen action.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub/Hugging Face)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes — only 518M parameters; extremely lightweight compared to competitors like MM Audio
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-04-01

## What it does
Analyzes video content and generates audio that matches the action — footsteps, guitar playing, crowd noise, explosions, etc. — timed precisely to what's happening on screen. Benchmarks show it outperforms larger models (MM Audio, Huayan Video) on multiple metrics despite being a fraction of the size. Best open-source video-to-audio tool currently available.

## When to use it
Adding sound effects to mute footage, AI-generated videos, or silent clips. Useful for animators, video editors, and game developers who need quick, plausible audio without a sound library or Foley session.
