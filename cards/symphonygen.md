# SymphonyGen

Open-source orchestral music generator that composes full symphonies from a harmony skeleton you can control.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes — models are under 5 MB
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-09

## What it does
Generates complete orchestral arrangements by first sketching a harmony skeleton (chords and direction), then filling in notes per instrument. You can supply your own harmony skeleton, start from a chosen chord, or extract the skeleton from an existing piece and generate a new arrangement inspired by it.

## When to use it
Orchestral/classical composition where you want harmonic control rather than a text-prompt black box. Also useful for generating variations that keep the chord pattern and tempo of a reference piece. Tiny enough to run on almost anything.
