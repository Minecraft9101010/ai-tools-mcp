# Wan Dancer

Generates long, consistent videos of any character dancing to any music you input, no reference choreography needed.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub, linked in video description)

- **Local-friendly:** no (total download is 85 GB; wait for quantized versions)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-19

## What it does
From the Alibaba Wan team. Takes a character image plus a music track and generates the character dancing in sync with the music, across genres from Chinese classical to Latin. Character stays consistent over much longer clips than other dance/animation tools, and optional keyframes let you control the choreography.

## When to use it
Virtual dancers, music videos, or character content where you have an image and a song but no motion reference. Unlike most dance animation tools, it doesn't need an existing dance video to copy movements from.
