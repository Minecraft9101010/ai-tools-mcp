# ChatGPT Health

ChatGPT feature that connects Apple Health and medical records so health questions get answered with your full history as context.

- **URL:** chatgpt.com (Health in the app sidebar)
- **Pricing:** freemium (rolling out to all plans, including free)
- **Platform:** web | mobile
- **Local-friendly:** no (cloud service)
- **Availability:** available (rolling out, US users 18+)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-26

## What it does
With permission, connects Apple Health plus supported medical records from certain US hospital systems and health apps. ChatGPT can then see medications, lab results, sleep, and exercise data, so answers about a new lab report or drug are tailored to your actual history rather than an isolated upload.

## When to use it
Interpreting lab results or medication questions where prior history changes the answer. Better than pasting a single report into a fresh chat, which lacks that context.
