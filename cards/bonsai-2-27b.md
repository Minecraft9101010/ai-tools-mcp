# Bonsai 2 27B

A heavily compressed open model derived from Qwen 3.8 27B.

- **URL:** https://prismml.com/news/bonsai-2-27b
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes - compressed versions are small enough for consumer GPUs and available through Hugging Face
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-20

## What it does
Bonsai 2 27B uses ternary weights to reduce the model footprint from about 55.6 GB to as low as about 5.9 GB. The video says it keeps performance close to the original full model while being much smaller.

## When to use it
Use it when you want a capable medium-sized local model that fits on lower-memory consumer GPUs.
