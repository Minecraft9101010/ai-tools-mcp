# Meta Claw

A framework that sits on top of OpenClaw (or other Claw variants) and makes AI agents get smarter automatically just by chatting with them over time.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (add-on layer, no additional GPU needed)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-03-27

## What it does
Intercepts your normal agent conversations, extracts learnable skills from them, and saves those skills to a growing library. On the next session, it injects relevant skills from the library into the agent's context, making it progressively better without retraining. Optionally runs reinforcement learning fine-tuning in the background during idle time.

## When to use it
Agentic workflows where you want your agent to learn your preferences and avoid recurring mistakes without manual fine-tuning — especially useful for OpenClaw-based setups.
