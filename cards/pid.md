# PID (NVIDIA)

Ultra-fast open-source image upscaler; version 1.5 improves detail sharpness and color fidelity over v1.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub, linked in video description)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (runs on a consumer GPU)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-19

## What it does
Turns a 512x512 latent image into a 2K image in a few seconds on a consumer GPU, described as the fastest open-source upscaler. Version 1.5 adds noticeably sharper details and better color fidelity than v1. Works with multiple image models, including latent image workflows, Flux, and Z Image.

## When to use it
Upscaling generated images at speed as part of a local pipeline, especially when throughput matters more than squeezing out maximum quality from slower super-resolution models.
