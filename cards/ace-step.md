# ACE-Step

Open-source music generator that outperforms Suno and Udio on quality benchmarks while running locally on consumer GPUs.

- **URL:** https://github.com/ace-step/ACE-Step
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes — runs on AMD, Nvidia, and Apple Silicon; 12 GB VRAM minimum with CPU offload, 20 GB recommended
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-04-10

## What it does
Generates full songs with vocals from a style prompt and lyrics. Supports instrumentals, multiple languages, and fine-grained control over instruments and song structure. Also supports a "thinking mode" with a paired language model for better compositional quality.

## When to use it
When you need royalty-free music generated locally without usage limits or per-generation costs. Especially useful if Suno/Udio's commercial terms are a concern or you want to run offline.
