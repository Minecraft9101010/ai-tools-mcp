## What it does
Instead of generating text or images, this model generates synthetic brain activity — what an fMRI scan would look like if a human watched a given video. Trained on hundreds of hours of fMRI data from 700+ people. Predicts real-time neural response patterns. Useful for neuroscience research, understanding how AI and human brains process information similarly or differently.

## When to use it
Neuroscience research, cognitive science studies, or AI interpretability work. Not a production tool — research-only at this stage.
