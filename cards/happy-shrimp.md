# Happy Shrimp

Music generator from the same lab behind Happy Horse — describe a style, optionally add lyrics, and get a clean, dynamic full song.

## What it does
Standard music-generator workflow: describe the song's style, enter lyrics or toggle instrumental mode. Output vocals and production sound notably clean and dynamic across genres.

## When to use it
Full songs with vocals when you want quality comparable to the best hosted music generators — and, at least for now, free. One of the strongest options currently usable.
