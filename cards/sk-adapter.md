# SK Adapter

A 3D modeling tool that generates full 3D objects from skeleton/rig structures — skeleton-conditioned generation for characters, vehicles, creatures, and mechanical objects.

## What it does
Upload a skeleton or rig structure and the model generates a full 3D mesh that conforms to it. Works across diverse categories: humanoid characters, birds, crustaceans, mechs, robots, spaceships. Training code and weights were released at time of coverage.

## When to use it
Game asset pipelines where you have rigged skeletons and need meshes, or animation workflows where maintaining a specific bone structure matters. Useful for rapid concept generation when you have a motion capture rig to constrain the output.
