## What it does
Instead of describing camera movement in text ("pan left"), you give it a reference video and a new source image; it learns the camera behavior and animates the image with it. Handles dramatic aerial and diving moves, multi-shot sequences with matching cuts (e.g., third-person then first-person view applied to a different subject), and special techniques like dolly zooms, bullet time, and fisheye distortion.

## When to use it
Replicating a specific cinematography style from existing footage, like applying an ad's exact camera language to different content. Watch for a release; nothing is usable yet.
