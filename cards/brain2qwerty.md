# Brain2Qwerty v2 (Meta)

Meta's non-invasive brain-to-text system: decodes MEG brain recordings into typed sentences, no surgery involved.

## What it does
Decodes magnetoencephalography (MEG) signals into text: version 2, trained on 10x more data than v1, reaches 69% character accuracy and 78% word accuracy. The point is typing by thinking with zero implants or surgery.

## When to use it
Brain-computer interface research and accessibility work where invasive implants are off the table. Accuracy isn't production-grade yet, but the released code makes it a usable research baseline.
