## What it does
Combines vision and language for agentic tasks — takes text, images, or video and reasons through what it sees. Demonstrated coding autonomously for 11 hours to build a full app, testing and iterating by analyzing its own screen. Beats similar-sized competitors (DeepSeek V4 Pro, GLM 5.1, Gemini 2.6) on average across agentic coding, reasoning, and knowledge benchmarks.
