## What it does
Generates multi-shot video sequences in a single inference pass — characters remain consistent across scene cuts with cinematic camera movement. Trained on real cinematic footage (not synthetic data), which the creators say is key to the quality. Outperforms competitors Hollow Scene and Multi-Shot Master on camera control and visual consistency.

## When to use it
Creating cinematic short-form content where you need multiple shots in sequence with a consistent character — trailers, social clips, brand videos — without editing separate generations together.
