# ChatGPT for financial services

An OpenAI financial-services product combining GPT models with financial datasets and tools for banking research workflows.

- **URL:** https://openai.com/index/introducing-chatgpt-financial-services/
- **Pricing:** paid
- **Platform:** web
- **Local-friendly:** no, it is a hosted product requiring sales access
- **Availability:** limited
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-13

## What it does
ChatGPT for financial services helps investigate companies, build financial models, and produce spreadsheets, documents, or presentations. It uses financial data providers and supports traceable figures and firm-specific templates.

## When to use it
Use it in finance teams that need AI-assisted company research, banking analysis, modeling, and report generation.
