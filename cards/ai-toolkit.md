# AI Toolkit (Ostris)

One of the best toolkits for training LoRAs on open image/video models, with early MiniMax H3 support.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes for the toolkit itself; training VRAM depends on the base model
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-05

## What it does
Trains LoRA fine-tunes (characters, styles, actions, effects) for open-source generative models. Added MiniMax H3 support within days of that model's release.

## When to use it
Training a custom character, style, or effect LoRA for MiniMax H3 or other open models rather than relying on community-published LoRAs. Technical; expect a real setup process.
