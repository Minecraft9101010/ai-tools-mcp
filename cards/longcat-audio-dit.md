# LongCat Audio DiT

Open-source TTS and voice cloning model from Meituan that generates speech in seconds and clones voices from short audio samples.

- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-04-05

## What it does
Text-to-speech generator with voice cloning from just a few seconds of reference audio. Multilingual. Benchmarks show it performs among the top TTS systems in terms of error rate and voice similarity. Created by Chinese food delivery company Meituan (same team behind LongCat image editing models).

## When to use it
Generating voiceovers in a specific speaker's voice, dubbing, or creating multilingual narration. Strong choice when you need fast, accurate voice cloning without a full voice acting studio.
