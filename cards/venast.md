# VenAST

Virtual try-on tool that animates clothing onto a person using pose skeleton video input.

## What it does
Takes a person image, clothing item image, and optional pose skeleton animation video; generates a realistic animation of that person wearing the clothing. Maintains body shape and facial consistency. Supports upper body, lower body, and full dress transfer.

## When to use it
Fashion e-commerce or content where you need to show clothing on a specific person without a photo shoot.
