# Hitem3D

All-in-one image-to-printable-3D platform: prompt or image to 3D model, then smart splitting, connectors, print-bed layout, and slicer export.

## What it does
Covers the full 3D printing workflow, not just generation: image to 3D model, automatic splitting into printable parts with connectors, print-bed arrangement and orientation suggestions, and two print modes (surface priority or less support). Offers multiple generation modes (fast ~2-3 min drafts, texture-focused, geometry-focused) with up to 1536 resolution, and lets you download models during the free trial.

## When to use it
Turning ideas or images into print-ready assets without knowing 3D modeling: miniatures, figures, game assets, product concepts. The splitting/connector pipeline is the differentiator over plain image-to-3D generators.
