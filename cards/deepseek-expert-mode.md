# DeepSeek Expert Mode

New reasoning-enhanced mode in DeepSeek's platform, possibly an early preview of DeepSeek V4 Light.

- **URL:** https://chat.deepseek.com
- **Pricing:** free (as of April 2025)
- **Platform:** web
- **Local-friendly:** no
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-01

## What it does
Unlocks noticeably deeper reasoning than standard DeepSeek V3. Stronger on tough logic puzzles, advanced math, coding, and multi-step problems. Lower hallucinations and higher accuracy than the default mode. Rumored to be a light preview of the upcoming V4.

## When to use it
Free alternative to paid reasoning models for hard math, logic, and coding tasks.
