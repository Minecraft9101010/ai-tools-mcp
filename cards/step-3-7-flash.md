# Step 3.7 Flash (StepFun)

Open-source efficient multimodal model built specifically for real-world agent work: reading interfaces, browsing, and staying coherent across long runs.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** no (~400GB, needs a DGX Spark or multiple GPUs)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-31

## What it does
A "flash" (efficient) multimodal model that takes text, images, documents, charts, and product interfaces as input, aimed at agentic use like browser automation and image/document analysis. Despite being a flash-tier model, it beats other open and closed flash models and approaches GPT-5.5 and Opus 4.7 on SWE-bench Pro, with strong multimodal and general agentic benchmark results.

## When to use it
Agentic workflows needing multimodal understanding (screenshots, documents, charts) at lower cost than a full flagship model — if you have the hardware to run a 400GB model.
