# Sega

Open-source high-resolution image generation/upscaling method that produces very large (4K-6K+), detailed, artifact-free images on top of Flux or Qwen Image.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** unknown
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-31

## What it does
Generates very high-resolution images (demoed up to 6,144px per side) with fine detail retained under zoom (water droplets, whiskers, building detail) and few visible errors. Works with Flux 1 or Qwen Image as a base; no confirmed support yet for Flux 2 or Z Image. Compared against other upscalers like DPIR, Sega produces more consistent, less error-prone results.

## When to use it
Producing very large, detailed final images from a Flux 1 or Qwen Image pipeline where a standard upscaler introduces visible artifacts at high zoom.
