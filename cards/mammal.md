## What it does
MAMMAL is a multimodal biological foundation model pre-trained on 2 billion samples spanning small molecules (SMILES strings), gene expression data, and protein/antibody sequences. It uses a modular tokenizer that maps each domain into a shared embedding space, letting the model reason across chemistry, genetics, and proteins simultaneously. It can predict drug toxicity, cell type identity, drug-cancer response, and design novel antibodies from scratch.

## When to use it
Drug repurposing research — finding new uses for existing drugs. Predicting whether a drug candidate will bind to a disease target. Designing antibodies for specific antigens. Useful for biosciences teams needing a single model that spans the full drug discovery pipeline rather than siloed specialist models.
