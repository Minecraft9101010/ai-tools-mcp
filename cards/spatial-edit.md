# Spatial Edit

Open-source image editing tool for precisely repositioning objects and adjusting camera angle in a photograph.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub plus model weights released; ~32 GB model)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (requires higher-end GPU)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-01

## What it does
Adjust exact 3D position and angle of any object in a photo, plus camera yaw, pitch, and zoom. Outperforms Nano Banana, Seedream, and Qwen Image Edit on object/camera placement benchmarks. Training data fully released.

## When to use it
Product photography recompositing, scene composition, or precise spatial control over objects without a full 3D render pipeline.
