## What it does
Odyssey 3 learns a broad visual world model and uses action decoders to translate that understanding into robot or agent control. The video shows applications across robot arms, humanoids, drones, autonomous driving, and simulated characters.

## When to use it
Use it for robotics or embodied-agent research where one model needs to generalize across different machines or environments.
