## What it does
Runway Agent takes a text description of a video concept and builds a full production plan before generating anything — every scene, visual direction, and story flow is shown upfront for review and editing. Once approved, it assembles the complete video: multiple shots, voiceover, music, and scene transitions. Aimed at reducing the gap between idea and finished content without manual timeline editing.

## When to use it
Product launches, ad campaigns, or YouTube intros where you want a finished multi-shot video without editing software. Good for small teams that need to produce more content faster. The pre-render plan review is the key differentiator over single-shot AI video tools.
