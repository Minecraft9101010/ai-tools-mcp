## What it does
Agent Native Research Artifacts (ARRA) replaces the PDF research paper format with a structured package readable by AI: a human-readable overview, the full logic graph (claims + experiments), complete code and configs, a graph of the research process including failed attempts, and raw evidence. The Live Research Manager is the accompanying agent that captures all of this automatically in the background as research happens — every experiment, every failure, every tweak — so the artifact builds itself. Improves AI understanding, reproducibility, and extension of the research compared to standard PDFs.

## When to use it
ML research teams who want to preserve the full research process, not just the final published story. Improving reproducibility when sharing work with collaborators or AI systems. Building research pipelines where AI agents will read and extend prior work programmatically.
