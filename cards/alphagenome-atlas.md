# AlphaGenome Atlas

A Google DeepMind searchable atlas of AI-predicted effects for billions of human genome variants.

- **URL:** https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-deepmind/alphagenome-atlas/
- **Pricing:** free
- **Platform:** web
- **Local-friendly:** no, it is described as an online atlas/database to search rather than a local tool
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-13

## What it does
AlphaGenome Atlas provides predictions for more than 9 billion genetic variants, including possible effects on gene regulation and protein production. Researchers can search the atlas instead of running predictions from scratch.

## When to use it
Use it for genetics research, variant interpretation, prioritizing mutations, or exploring possible disease associations.
