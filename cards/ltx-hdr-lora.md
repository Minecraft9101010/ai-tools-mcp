# LTX HDR LoRA

Lightweight LoRA (340 MB) from LTX that converts any LTX video generation to full HDR, dramatically improving dynamic range and color depth.

- **URL:** not mentioned (from LTX; add-on to LTX workflow)

- **Local-friendly:** yes (340 MB LoRA; adds to existing LTX workflow)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-04-26

## What it does
LTX HDR LoRA is a lightweight add-on for the LTX video generator (the best open-source video generator with sound). Previous LTX outputs were 8-bit SDR with limited dynamic range. Adding this LoRA converts output to full HDR, improving color balance and giving more headroom for post-processing color grading in a video editor. Only 340 MB.

## When to use it
Any LTX video generation workflow where you plan to do color grading or compositing afterward. Improving the look of AI-generated video that feels flat or oversaturated in SDR. Plug into existing LTX workflows without any model replacement.
