# Video MDM

Generates 3D human motion from text prompts, trained entirely from ordinary 2D videos with no mocap data.

## What it does
Extracts accurate body poses from 2D video to train a model that generates coherent 3D human motion from text: "a person waving with his right hand," "walking backwards in a straight line," plus gym movements like deadlifts, push-ups, and barbell rows. Sidesteps the scarcity of 3D mocap training data.

## When to use it
Text-to-motion for animation, games, or robotics pipelines where you need 3D human motion clips without a mocap setup. MIT license.
