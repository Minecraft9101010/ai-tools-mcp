# Solaris (Multiplayer Minecraft Video Gen)

Open-source AI that generates synthetic first-person Minecraft gameplay video from two simultaneous player perspectives.

- **URL:** https://github.com (search Solaris Minecraft)
- **Pricing:** open-source (free)
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (open-source; ~29.2GB, needs high-end GPU)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-03-05

## What it does
Solaris generates realistic synthetic Minecraft video from two player viewpoints simultaneously, keeping both views consistent across actions like combat, building, mining, weather, and inventory changes. Trained on 6.32M frames per player from a custom Solaris Engine. Represents a step toward multi-agent world model training.

## When to use it
Research or experimentation in multi-agent world models, generating synthetic gameplay data, or studying AI game engine development. Practical model size requires a high-end GPU.
