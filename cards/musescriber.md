# MuseScriber

Open-source music transcription: upload a song, get it dissected into per-instrument MIDI tracks, vocals included.

- **URL:** not mentioned (HuggingFace, with a free Space demo)
- **Pricing:** open-source (free hosted Space available)
- **Platform:** web | local
- **Local-friendly:** yes — large variant is 5.5 GB, small (0.1B) is 412 MB and could run on a phone
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-15

## What it does
Transcribes a full mixed song into separate MIDI tracks per detected instrument (including voice) in seconds. You can optionally pre-specify the instruments present to improve accuracy. Output imports into any DAW for correction and remixing. Three sizes: large, medium, small.

## When to use it
Reverse-engineering a song's melody, chords, or arrangement — sampling, remixing, learning a part, or extracting a harmony skeleton. Expect some prediction errors to clean up in the DAW.
