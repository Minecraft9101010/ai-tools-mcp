# Olive (Brain Ultrasound Imaging)

Open-sourced ultrasound imaging pipeline that maps blood flow in a living human brain in 3D at roughly 100x the resolution of comparable CT scans, without drilling into the skull.

- **URL:** not mentioned
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local (requires lab-grade ultrasound plus microbubble contrast equipment)
- **Local-friendly:** no (needs specialized medical hardware)
- **Availability:** available (pipeline and datasets open-sourced; requires lab access to actually run)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-28

## What it does
Uses ultrasound plus injected microbubble contrast agents to track blood flow through brain vessels — sitting between invasive electrode implants (precise, invasive) and external EEG (safe, blurry). No skull drilling, though the injected contrast bubbles still carry some risk the source material doesn't fully address. This week the team captured the most detailed vascular map of a living human brain to date and open-sourced the full pipeline, including datasets.

## When to use it
Research groups with lab access wanting a non-drilling but high-resolution method for mapping brain blood flow — a step toward understanding brain function or diseases like Alzheimer's, not a consumer tool.
