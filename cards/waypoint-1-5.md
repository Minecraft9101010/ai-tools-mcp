# Waypoint 1.5 (Overworld)

Open-source real-time interactive world model that runs on consumer GPUs at up to 720p/60fps.

- **URL:** not mentioned (open-source download; biome.exe installer)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (RTX 3070 minimum; Apple Silicon support planned)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-01

## What it does
Generates real-time interactive 3D worlds you can walk through and interact with. Runs at 720p/60fps on higher-end hardware and 360p on lower-end hardware. Lower visual quality than cloud-based alternatives but runs entirely locally.

## When to use it
Experimenting with real-time interactive AI world generation locally. The most hardware-accessible open-source real-time world generator available.
