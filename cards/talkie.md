## What it does
Talkie is a contamination-free research model trained exclusively on pre-1930 text. It has no concept of computers, the internet, or anything post-1930. Despite this, it can partially generalize to new concepts from just a few in-context examples (e.g., learned to write simple Python functions from examples alone, with zero prior training on code). Useful for studying how training data shapes model behavior, personality, and knowledge representation. Researchers can study generalization and forecasting without modern data contamination.

## When to use it
AI research into how training data shapes model behavior and knowledge representation. Testing generalization in a clean, contamination-free environment. Historical research or education contexts where interacting with a pre-modern knowledge base is valuable. Chat with it for novelty, but the real value is research.
