# Surf Flow

Fuses multiple photos of a scene into one clean 3D model via a shared global 3D state, even with unaligned images and unknown camera positions.

## What it does
Maintains one compact global understanding of a scene that improves as you add views, using extra photos to fill in missing parts of the 3D model rather than carrying redundant image information. Works without camera pose data or aligned inputs.

## When to use it
Multi-image 3D scene reconstruction from casual, unposed photo sets, once released. Watch the project page.
