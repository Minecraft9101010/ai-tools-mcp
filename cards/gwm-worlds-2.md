# GWM Worlds 2

GWM Worlds 2 is Runway's research preview for generating interactive virtual worlds in real time.

- **URL:** https://runway.com/research/introducing-gwm-worlds-2
- **Pricing:** unknown
- **Platform:** web
- **Local-friendly:** no, the transcript says it is a research preview requiring a form and is not generally available.
- **Availability:** limited
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-06

## What it does
It generates continuous 720p, 24 fps interactive worlds from an image or description, allowing movement and actions through commands. It uses an autoregressive diffusion approach for video and audio with no fixed maximum duration.

## When to use it
Use it, when available, for interactive generative environments, game-like world exploration, and real-time controllable scene generation.
