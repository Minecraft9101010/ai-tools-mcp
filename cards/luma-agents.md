## What it does
Luma Agents understands the physical world behind creative work (motion, space, object behavior) and orchestrates AI agents across video, design, and planning tasks. Instead of switching between tools, you work in one canvas where agents route tasks behind the scenes. Positioned for designers, content creators, and creative teams who want to explore ideas at scale without managing tool switching.

## When to use it
Full creative pipeline work where you're currently juggling multiple tools (video, design, planning, communication). Best when you bring your own creative direction and want the agents to handle execution and routing.
