## What it does
Splits a long video into chunks, predicts depth and camera position per chunk, then stitches and adjusts the chunks at inference until the whole sequence agrees on one consistent 3D world — using nearby frames, overlapping views, and long-range matches. Lowest average error across the reconstruction benchmarks shown.
