## What it does
Gene 26.5 is Genesis AI's robot foundation model for fine-grained physical manipulation — cracking eggs one-handed, chopping vegetables, operating pipettes in centrifuge workflows, solving Rubik's cubes with continuous 360-degree rotation, and playing piano. Built with a human-scale dexterous robotic hand and a new data engine that captures human skills and converts them into robot training data. The open-source platform "Genesis" (a virtual physics simulation gym for robot training) is available separately.

## When to use it
Not yet publicly available. The Genesis simulation platform is open-source and relevant now for robotics researchers training models in simulation. Gene 26.5 itself is worth tracking for teams working on dexterous manipulation research.
