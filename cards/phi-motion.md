# Phi Motion

Reward system that scores physical plausibility of human movement in AI-generated video.

- **URL:** not mentioned

- **Local-friendly:** unknown
- **Availability:** available

## What it does
Reconstructs a 3D body from video, runs a physics simulation, and scores the output on joint behavior and ground contact. Designed to catch physically impossible poses that video generators produce. Used as a reward signal during training or as an evaluation metric.

## When to use it
Fixing or filtering AI-generated video where people do complex movements (sports, dance, stunts) and the physics looks wrong.
