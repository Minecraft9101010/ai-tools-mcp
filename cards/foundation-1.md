# Foundation 1

Free, open-source AI model for music production that generates individual instrument stems (with MIDI export) at a specified BPM, key, and bar count.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub)
- **Pricing:** free / open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (works with low VRAM; ~15 seconds per stem on RTX 5000 Ada with 16 GB VRAM)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-03-25

## What it does
Generates musically coherent audio loops for individual instruments — synths, bass, flute, trumpet, strings, kalimba, etc. — following BPM, key, bar count, and detailed prompt descriptors (reverb, delay, distortion, timbre, wave shape, arpeggio, staccato, etc.). Outputs audio files and MIDI files (so you can reassign the notes to any virtual instrument). Designed for stem-by-stem production: you generate each instrument separately and mix them in a DAW. Does not generate drums or percussion.

## When to use it
Music producers who want AI-generated stems they can actually mix and master, rather than full-song outputs they can't edit. Especially useful if you want to control the sonic character of each instrument closely through prompting.
