# Gemini Omni

Google's multimodal model for AI-driven video generation and editing.

- **URL:** gemini.google.com and Google Flow
- **Pricing:** freemium (Pro users+)
- **Platform:** web
- **Local-friendly:** no (cloud-only processing)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-21

## What it does
Takes text, image, video, or audio as input and generates or edits video output. Supports style transfers, background swaps, object removal, camera angle changes, and audio-synced effects.

## When to use it
AI video content creation, editing footage with AI effects, producing educational explainers, or replacing objects in existing clips.
