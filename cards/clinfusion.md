# ClinFusion (Alibaba DAMO)

Open-source medical multimodal model: X-rays, scans, and native 3D imaging plus text in; medical answers and clinical reports out.

- **URL:** not mentioned (HuggingFace / project page)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** partially — 8B version is 24 GB (mid-to-high-end GPU); 32B version is 72 GB (no)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-09

## What it does
A unified vision encoder handles heterogeneous medical imaging (2D and 3D modalities) in one system, answering medical questions and generating clinical reports. Beats leading open and closed models on most medical multimodal benchmarks, including the older GPT-5.2. Ships in 32B (higher quality) and 8B sizes.

## When to use it
Medical imaging analysis or report drafting where data can't leave your infrastructure — likely the best open medical model in its size range. The 8B version is the practical local pick.
