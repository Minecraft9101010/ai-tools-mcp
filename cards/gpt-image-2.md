# GPT Image 2 (OpenAI)

OpenAI's best image generator, capable of full diagrams, infographics, photorealistic images, and complex multi-element scenes with far fewer errors than previous models.

- **URL:** https://chatgpt.com
- **Pricing:** paid (ChatGPT subscription or API)
- **Platform:** web | API
- **Local-friendly:** no (cloud only)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-04-26

## What it does
GPT Image 2 (also called ChatGPT Images 2) generates highly detailed, accurate images including grids of 100 posters with correct details, Windows 11 desktop screenshots with readable text in Slack and Excel, complex multi-element infographics, and photorealistic photography. Significantly fewer errors than the previous leader Ideogram. Strongest closed-source image model at time of review.

## When to use it
Generating complex images that require accurate text rendering, multiple coherent elements, or photorealistic results. Infographics, UI mockups, product visuals, detailed posters. When accuracy and detail matter more than cost.
