# Cosmos 3 (NVIDIA)

Open-source foundation model for physical AI — robots, autonomous vehicles, and anything that needs to understand the real world from text/image/video/audio/action and generate what happens next.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub/HuggingFace)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** no (nano variant ~35GB, super variant ~130GB)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-07

## What it does
Combines text, image, video, audio, and action inputs to understand a scene and predict/generate what happens next — used to generate synthetic training video for autonomous driving or robot manipulation. Ships in "nano" and "super" sizes, with model weights, training scripts, deployment tools, and training datasets (robotics, physical interactions, driving, warehouse scenes) all released.

## When to use it
Generating synthetic training video for autonomous driving models or robotics when you don't have enough real-world footage, or building physical-world simulation pipelines.
