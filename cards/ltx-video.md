# LTX Video

Open-source local video generator with native audio, built by Lightricks.

- **URL:** https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-Video
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local | API
- **Local-friendly:** yes — runs on low VRAM consumer GPUs; 2.3 weights downloadable from HuggingFace
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-03-15

## What it does
Generates video from text or image prompts with audio baked in natively — no separate audio step. Version 2.3 (March 2026) adds first-frame/last-frame control, vertical format support, better motion consistency, and improved lip-sync for multilingual dialogue.

## When to use it
Best free local option when you need video with integrated audio and can't pay for Kling or Runway. Strong for dialogue-heavy or action scenes where motion coherence matters. Struggles with dramatic audio effects (explosions, etc.).
