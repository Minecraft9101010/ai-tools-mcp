# Dot TTS

Small open-source text-to-speech model with zero-shot voice cloning from a few seconds of reference audio, including stuttering, whispering, and cross-lingual output.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub)
- **Pricing:** open-source (Apache 2)
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (2B parameters, ~5GB base model fits most consumer GPUs)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-14

## What it does
Clones a voice zero-shot from a short sample and reads out any transcript, reproducing subtle delivery like stutters, whispers, gasps, and sighs. Speaks multiple languages even when the reference voice doesn't. Sits in the best corner of the error-rate vs voice-similarity chart, beating recent TTS models like Voxy PM, Qwen 3 TTS, and Index TTS 2.

## When to use it
Local voice cloning and narration on consumer hardware: audiobooks, character voices with emotional delivery, multilingual dubs of a single voice. Apache 2 license permits commercial use.
