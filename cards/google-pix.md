# Google Pix

AI image creation and editing built into Google Workspace apps.

- **URL:** not mentioned (Workspace)
- **Pricing:** paid (Pro/Ultra, rolling out globally summer 2026)
- **Platform:** web
- **Local-friendly:** no (cloud-based)
- **Availability:** limited (rolling out)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-21

## What it does
Lets you select specific objects within an image in Docs, Slides, or Drive, then move, resize, or transform them without regenerating the whole image.

## When to use it
Editing images inside presentations without opening a separate app, making precise adjustments to AI-generated images, or doing visual work without leaving Docs or Slides.
