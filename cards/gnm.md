# GNM (Google)

Parametric 3D head and face designer: hundreds of sliders for identity, expression, gaze, teeth, and tongue output a dense head mesh.

- **URL:** not mentioned (linked in video description)
- **Pricing:** open-source (Apache 2, commercial use allowed)
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** unknown
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-19

## What it does
A generative anthropometric head model with 253 identity controls (170 head, 80 teeth) and 383 expression controls (100 per eye, 150 lower face, 32 tongue). Each control is independent, so you can adjust one facial feature without affecting the rest, and it outputs a dense 3D head mesh matching your exact settings.

## When to use it
Designing unique, precisely specified character faces instead of the samey faces image generators default to. Useful for character pipelines that need repeatable, adjustable head geometry down to internal structures like eyeballs and gums.
