# MiDashengLM-Gen (Xiaomi)

Open-source unified audio scene generator that blends speech, music, sound effects, and environmental sound from a single text description.

- **URL:** https://github.com/xiaomi-research/midashenglm-gen

- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-16

## What it does
Generates complete audio scenes — speech (with emotion and multiple languages), music, sound effects, and environmental noise — combined in a single generation from a text description, rather than requiring separate tools per audio type. Not a dedicated music or TTS model, so quality trails specialists in each category, but it's the only one handling all of them together.

## When to use it
One-shot generation of composite audio scenes (dialogue over ambient sound plus music, for example) where using separate specialist tools per layer would be overkill.
