# Gamma

AI tool for creating presentations, websites, and social content, with native Claude connector integration.

- **URL:** https://gamma.app
- **Pricing:** freemium
- **Platform:** web
- **Local-friendly:** no (cloud-only)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-03-05

## What it does
Gamma generates polished presentations from text or data. As of early 2026, it has a native Claude connector so you can summon it directly inside Claude conversations to turn Claude outputs into decks without copying. It can pull from Gmail, Google Drive, Google Calendar, GitHub, HubSpot, and Notion as data sources.

## When to use it
Turning research or AI-generated content into shareable presentations quickly, without leaving your Claude workflow. Also useful for auto-generating sales reports or converting Notion docs into slides.
