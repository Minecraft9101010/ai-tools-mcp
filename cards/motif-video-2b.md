# Motif Video 2B

Super-lightweight 2B parameter video diffusion transformer that rivals larger models while using ~10x less training data.

- **URL:** not mentioned (HuggingFace released)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (min 19 GB VRAM with CPU offloading; ComfyUI support coming)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-01

## What it does
Generates diverse video scenes and art styles. Used under 100K GPU hours and fewer than 10M training videos to train — roughly 7x fewer parameters and 10x less data than Wan 2.1, yet performs close to Wan 2.2 on V-Bench and outperforms older heavier models like Wan 2.1, HunYuan Video, and Step Video.

## When to use it
When you want a capable open-source video generator that doesn't require enterprise-level GPU resources to run or fine-tune.
