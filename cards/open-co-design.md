# Open Co-design

Open-source, self-hosted AI design agent that builds UIs, slide decks, PDFs, and posters from text descriptions or reference images — a free alternative to Claude Co-design and Lovable.

- **URL:** not mentioned (open source; simple EXE installer)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** desktop app
- **Local-friendly:** yes (EXE installer; plug in your own model)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-04-26

## What it does
Open Co-design takes a text description or reference images and autonomously builds user interfaces, slide decks, PDFs, posters, and other visual designs. You can plug in your own AI model. Works as a self-hosted alternative to proprietary design agents. Relatively easy install (single EXE). Live preview panel shows the generated design alongside the agent's work.

## When to use it
Teams that want AI-assisted design without paying for Claude Co-design or Lovable. When you need to self-host for privacy or cost reasons. Rapid UI mockups or deck generation from a brief description. Plugging in a local or custom model for design generation.
