# Just Dub It

Video dubbing with matching lip sync in the target language.

- **URL:** not mentioned

- **Local-friendly:** yes (2.5GB model, runs on mid-range GPU)
- **Availability:** available

## What it does
Dubs video into another language and re-syncs the speaker's lip movements to match. Built on a fine-tuned LoRA on LTX 2.3. Model weight is 2.5GB.

## When to use it
Dubbing video into another language with matching lip sync, without paying HeyGen rates.
