# ChatGPT Desktop Live Voice

Hands-free voice control of the ChatGPT desktop app: talk to coordinate multiple projects and files on your computer at once.

- **URL:** not mentioned (ChatGPT desktop app)
- **Pricing:** paid (desktop live voice is for paid plans; the desktop app itself is free)
- **Platform:** desktop app
- **Local-friendly:** no (cloud service)
- **Availability:** available (Mac first; not yet on the Windows app)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-26

## What it does
Brings GPT live voice, previously web-only, into the ChatGPT desktop app. You can speak to the app to coordinate tasks across multiple local projects and files simultaneously instead of typing into per-project threads. One voice chat active at a time, and voice usage draws on plan credits.

## When to use it
Vibe-coding or managing several projects hands-free while doing other things; effectively a voice control layer for increasingly complicated ChatGPT desktop work.
