## What it does
Text-to-speech with optional voice cloning. Key differentiator: emotion and delivery tags can be embedded directly in the transcript — things like [inhale], [emphasis], [pause], [laugh], [whisper], [scream], [shout]. This gives fine-grained expressive control over the output. Output sounds natural with emotional nuance.

## When to use it
When you need expressive, emotionally-controlled speech synthesis — podcasts, audiobook production, character dialogue, social content voiceover. The tag system is more approachable than SSML. Open-source means it can be self-hosted.
