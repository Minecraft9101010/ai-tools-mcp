# Helios

Real-time open-source video generator that produces long videos on a single GPU.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub available)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** no — requires H100 GPU for the 19.5fps performance; too large for most consumer GPUs
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-03-13

## What it does
Generates videos in real time at 19.5 frames per second on a single H100, capable of producing clips up to one minute long. Best real-time quality demonstrated as of March 2026 — on par with non-real-time models like LTX2 and LongCat Video on quality benchmarks. Training code also released.

## When to use it
Research or production environments with H100 access that need fast iteration on long video clips. Not practical for consumer setups yet, but best available real-time option with quality that isn't sacrificed.
