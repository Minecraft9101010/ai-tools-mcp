# Lyra 2 (Nvidia)

Nvidia's open-source 3D world generator that converts video into explorable interactive 3D environments via Gaussian splatting.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub released, ~131 MB)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (131 MB model, surprisingly small)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-01

## What it does
Takes a raw video and converts it into a 3D point cloud / Gaussian splat — a persistent, explorable 3D environment. The reconstructed scene stays consistent long-horizon even if you look away and return. Exports are compatible with Nvidia Isaac Sim for robot training in simulated environments.

## When to use it
Creating simulation-ready 3D environments for robot training, or generating explorable scenes from video footage for spatial AI, AR, or digital twins.
