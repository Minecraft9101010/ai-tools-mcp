# Higgs Audio V3

Open-source TTS with inline prompt tags for controlling emotion, speaking style, speed, pitch, pauses, and sound effects mid-sentence.

## What it does
Generates speech from text with inline tags that control delivery — emotion, style, speed, pitch, pauses, and sound effects — inside the same sentence. Outperforms other leading open-source TTS models on average in Stability's comparison.

## When to use it
TTS work needing fine-grained, in-line control over delivery (tone shifts mid-sentence, sound effects) rather than a single flat emotional setting.
