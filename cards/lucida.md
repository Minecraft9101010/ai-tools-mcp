# Lucida

Open-source background remover that handles the hard cases: glass, transparency, camouflage, hair, text, shadows, and glow.

- **URL:** not mentioned (HuggingFace demo + model page, linked in video description)
- **Pricing:** open-source (MIT license)
- **Platform:** local | web (free HuggingFace demo)
- **Local-friendly:** yes (under 900 MB, fits most consumer GPUs)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-19

## What it does
Removes backgrounds while preserving details competitors miss: it keeps transparency in glass and water, separates text from its shadow, handles camouflaged subjects, and cleans fine hair edges better than Ideogram and other removal models. Model, training details, and dataset are all released. The free demo processes an image in about 6 seconds.

## When to use it
Background removal where the subject involves transparency, fine hair, glow, or subject/background similarity that breaks standard removers. MIT license makes it safe to embed in commercial pipelines.
