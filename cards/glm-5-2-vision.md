# GLM 5.2 Vision (Baseten, unofficial)

Community build that bolts a Kimi vision encoder onto GLM 5.2, giving the text-only open model eyes.

- **URL:** not mentioned (HuggingFace, linked in video description)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** no (NVFP4 quantized version is still 466 GB; needs multiple DGX Spark-class machines)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-26

## What it does
Baseten merged a vision encoder created by the Kimi team into GLM 5.2's architecture, producing an unofficial vision-capable variant of one of the leading open models. Not published or endorsed by the ZAI or Kimi teams. Distributed as an NVFP4 quantization.

## When to use it
When you already run GLM 5.2 locally and need image understanding without switching model families, and you have the enterprise-class hardware to fit it.
