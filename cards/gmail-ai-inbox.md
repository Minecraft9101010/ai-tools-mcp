# Gmail AI Inbox

AI-powered Gmail upgrade that surfaces important items and pre-drafts replies.

- **URL:** gmail.com (Workspace update)
- **Pricing:** freemium (Pro/Ultra and business tiers first)
- **Platform:** web
- **Local-friendly:** no (cloud-based)
- **Availability:** limited (rolling out)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-21

## What it does
Surfaces important updates, tasks, and files from your inbox. Generates personalized reply drafts and proactively surfaces relevant attachments from previous threads.

## When to use it
Triaging a flooded inbox, getting pre-drafted replies ready to send, or finding buried attachments without searching manually.
