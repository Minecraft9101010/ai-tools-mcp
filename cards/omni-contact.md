# OmniContact

Framework that teaches humanoid robots long, chained whole-body tasks (carry, push, kick, recover) via a "Contact Flow" plan of body motion and contact points.

- **URL:** not mentioned (code and dataset released, linked in video description)

- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-05

## What it does
Plans how a robot's body should move and where it should make contact over time, letting it chain skills smoothly: walk, bend, grab a box, carry it, push another, recover from losing balance. Near-100% success on box carrying, sustains 40-minute multi-step tasks, and integrates with vision models for semantic goals like arranging boxes into shapes. Training dataset is released alongside the code.

## When to use it
Humanoid robot research needing loco-manipulation beyond isolated single-skill demos, or as a released dataset for training your own whole-body policies.
