# Gemini Spark

Google's 24/7 personal cloud agent that keeps running after you close your laptop.

- **URL:** not mentioned (Workspace beta)
- **Pricing:** paid (Ultra subscribers, beta)
- **Platform:** web
- **Local-friendly:** no (cloud-based by design)
- **Availability:** limited (beta, Ultra only)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-21

## What it does
Runs on Gemini 3.5 as a persistent agent. Monitors tasks, tracks information over time, and executes multi-step workflows across Google Workspace apps without requiring you to be present.

## When to use it
Monitoring inbox for recurring updates, running background research while you're offline, or synthesizing scattered notes into a finished Doc overnight.
