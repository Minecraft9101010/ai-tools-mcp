## What it does
Turns the mouse cursor into a Gemini-powered assistant that reads the content under it. Hover over text, images, or other content and ask questions without opening a separate chat window. Being integrated into Chrome and Google Books.

## When to use it
Querying or summarizing content you're already looking at without copy-pasting into a chatbot. Watch for Chrome extension availability.
