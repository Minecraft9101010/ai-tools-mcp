## What it does
Suno V6 creates songs from text prompts or audio references and supports prompt-based song edits. The video says paid plans get V6 and V6 Wild, while V6 mini is available to everyone including free users.

## When to use it
Use it for web-based AI music generation, song ideation, lyric edits, remixes, and controlled song variations.
