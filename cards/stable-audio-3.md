# Stable Audio 3 (Stability AI)

Open-source music and audio generation model with inpainting, extension, and LoRA fine-tuning support.

- **URL:** not mentioned (HuggingFace)
- **Pricing:** freemium (small/medium open-source, large API-only)
- **Platform:** local | API
- **Local-friendly:** yes (medium ~10GB, up to 6:20 tracks)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-24

## What it does
Generates music and sound effects from text prompts. Medium variant (1.4B params, ~10GB) generates tracks up to 6 minutes 20 seconds and is fully open-source. Supports audio inpainting, track extension, and LoRA fine-tuning. Large model is API-only.

## When to use it
Generating background music or SFX for video, audio inpainting to fix gaps in tracks, fine-tuning custom audio styles, or replacing paid music licensing for projects that can run ~10GB locally.
