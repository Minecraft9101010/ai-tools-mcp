# Lab OS

AI co-scientist system that connects AI reasoning to physical lab work via XR smart glasses, enabling real-time guidance during experiments.

## What it does
Lab OS is a two-part system: a dry lab side where AI agents help plan experiments and analyze data, and a physical lab side where XR smart glasses let the AI see what the researcher is doing in real time. The AI tracks the experiment step-by-step, identifies objects in the field of view, and can warn the researcher before they make a mistake. Captures tacit lab skills (pipetting angle, timing cues) that are hard to communicate via chat.

## When to use it
Wet lab research where protocol adherence matters and mistakes are expensive. Training junior researchers with AI-guided supervision. Any lab setting where hands-free real-time guidance during an experiment would reduce errors.
