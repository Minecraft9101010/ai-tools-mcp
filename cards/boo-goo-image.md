# Boo Goo Image

Open-source image generator and editor in the Nano Banana / GPT Image mold, with a permissive Apache 2 license its rivals lack.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub; ComfyUI support available)
- **Pricing:** open-source (Apache 2)
- **Platform:** local (ComfyUI)
- **Local-friendly:** yes (base and edit models 20GB full / 10GB FP8; medium-to-high-end GPU)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-21

## What it does
Generates from text and edits with reference images: photorealism, text/infographics/posters, good anatomy, and strong world knowledge of logos, celebrities, and app interfaces (can fake an Instagram profile screenshot). Its own arena claims wins over Z Image, Qwen, and HiDream, but the reviewer's hands-on found it behind Z Image for photorealism and Flux Klein for editing, and slower than Flux. Comes as base, edit, and 4-step turbo variants.

## When to use it
When license matters: the top open image models (Ideogram 4, Flux Klein) carry restrictive or non-commercial licenses, and this is the permissive Apache 2 alternative for commercial image generation and editing. For pure quality, Z Image or Flux Klein still win.
