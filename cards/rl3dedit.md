## What it does
Edits full 3D scenes via natural language prompts — change character expressions, add or remove objects, alter materials, change artistic style, replace backgrounds. Works on full Gaussian Splatting scenes, not just 2D images. Outperforms other 3D editing methods on quality and speed.

## When to use it
3D scene authoring and modification when you have a 3D scene (e.g. Gaussian Splat) and want to make changes without re-capturing or manually modeling. Useful for game previs, VR world editing, or architectural visualization iteration.
