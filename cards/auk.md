# AuK

A Tencent audio model for text-to-speech, voice cloning, speech editing, denoising, enhancement, and emotion or timbre changes.

- **URL:** https://auk-project.github.io/
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes, the transcript says it can run locally and the model is about 6.12 GB
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-13

## What it does
AuK can generate speech from text, clone voices from short samples, edit existing speech by adding or deleting words, remove noise, enhance audio, and modify emotion or timbre. The video describes it as a highly flexible speech editing tool.

## When to use it
Use it for local speech generation, voice editing, audio cleanup, voice style changes, or content creation workflows.
