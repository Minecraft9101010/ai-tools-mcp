## What it does
MapToWorld takes a colored segment map and a text prompt describing what each colored area should be (e.g., "blue = spring village, yellow = ancient Rome") and generates a fully navigable 3D world matching that layout. The world is explorable and rotatable. You can also control building scale and density. Different zones maintain their assigned aesthetics within the same generated world.

## When to use it
Concept art and world-building for games or simulation. Rapid 3D environment prototyping from a high-level map design. Useful for anyone who wants to translate a 2D zone map into a 3D explorable space without manual 3D modeling.
