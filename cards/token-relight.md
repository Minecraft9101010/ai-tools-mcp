# Token Relight (Adobe)

Adobe research model for precise continuous lighting control on photographs — adjusts intensity, color, 3D position, and diffuse quality.

## What it does
Tokenizes lighting attributes (intensity, diffuse color, 3D position, ambient illumination) and feeds them through a transformer for realistic photo relighting. Gives continuous slider-like control — move a light source in 3D, change its color, adjust diffuse vs. sharp, or modify ambient lighting — and the image updates realistically including shadows and highlights. Training-free light-geometry reasoning emerges from the attribute-tokenized training approach.

## When to use it
Product photography retouching, creative compositing, or any workflow needing precise light repositioning without a full 3D render. Currently research-only.
