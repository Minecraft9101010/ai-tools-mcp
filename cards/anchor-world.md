# Anchor World

First-person world simulator driven by real human motion: input 3D body movement plus anchor images, get an egocentric video of that person moving through the scene.

## What it does
Instead of prompts or keypresses, you guide the simulator with a person's 3D motion through a scene, anchored by reference views of how parts of the scene should look. Renders first-person video including object interaction: operating a sink, holding a knife.

## When to use it
Generating egocentric training video for humanoid robots or embodied agents, once the code releases.
