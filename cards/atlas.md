# Atlas

Atlas is World Labs' world model for creating or reconstructing coherent 3D worlds from text, images, video, or 3D inputs.

- **Pricing:** unknown

- **Availability:** limited
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-06

## What it does
It can generate coherent 1440p video from a camera path, reconstruct real places from photos, and output 3D geometry like point clouds or Gaussian splats. It is positioned for world reconstruction, simulation, VR, and synthetic data.

## When to use it
Use it when you need high-fidelity 3D world reconstruction, camera-path video generation, real estate VR, or simulation data generation.
