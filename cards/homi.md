# Homi

Reference-to-video generator that keeps multiple people and complex objects faithful to your input images, with multi-view support.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub, linked in video description)
- **Pricing:** open-source (Apache 2, commercial use allowed)
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** no (37 GB total; high-end GPU or wait for quantized versions)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-26

## What it does
Generates videos containing the specific characters and objects you input as references, photorealistic or 3D-animated. Its differentiators: you can supply multiple views of a character or object for stronger consistency at all angles, and an optional OCR map tells it where text should appear on objects for accurate text rendering. Built on Wan 2.1 and Phantom; more faithful with fewer errors than earlier reference-to-video models like VACE and Phantom, especially for complex designs.

## When to use it
Mass-producing UGC/influencer-style product videos from a person photo plus product shots, or any reference-driven video where intricate designs (patterned clothing, branded products, text on packaging) must stay consistent.
