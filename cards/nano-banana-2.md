# Nano Banana 2

Google's latest image generation and editing model, faster and cheaper than its predecessor with better world understanding.

- **URL:** https://gemini.google.com | https://aistudio.google.com
- **Pricing:** free (Gemini app); API access requires paid key (via AI Studio)
- **Platform:** web | API
- **Local-friendly:** no (cloud-only)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-03-03

## What it does
Nano Banana 2 (based on Gemini 3.1 Flash) generates and edits images with strong celebrity/character likeness, complex emotion grids, and multi-reference inputs (up to 14 objects, 5 characters per prompt). Supports 4K resolution and four new panoramic aspect ratios (4:1, 8:1). Generates in seconds vs. the slower Nano Banana Pro.

## When to use it
Fast image generation where quality is important but wait time matters. Best for text-to-image and multi-character scenes. For complex editing tasks with lots of text or data tables, Nano Banana Pro still edges it out.
