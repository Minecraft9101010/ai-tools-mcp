# Scale 2

Open-source motion transfer for video: drives one or more characters in a new video with the movement from a reference video, by ZAI.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub / Hugging Face)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** no (full model set is 81GB; wait for quantized/GGUF versions)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-14

## What it does
Transfers motion from a driving video onto different characters, including multiple characters at once, non-human animals, and creatures with abnormal proportions. Handles realistic footage, anime, and artistic styles, and captures the driving video's camera movement. Quality is roughly on par with closed-source Kling 3 and clearly ahead of Wan Animate and the first Scale.

## When to use it
Character replacement or motion-driven animation from reference footage: swapping a video's actor for a different character, animating a creature with a human performance, or multi-character action scenes. Best open-source pick for this as of June 2026 if you have the hardware or can wait for quantized versions.
