# PhysFormer

Predicts physically realistic motion and deformation of 3D objects (dropping, squishing, collisions) from a mesh plus material and initial conditions.

## What it does
Takes a starting 3D mesh, position, velocity, and material type (rigid vs elastic) and generates physically realistic animations of how the objects move, bend, and interact over time. Trained on 100K+ simulated trajectories; beats older methods on physical accuracy, generalizes to unseen shapes, and handles many objects at once.

## When to use it
Adding believable physics to generated 3D assets, which most AI 3D generators leave rigid: simulations, physically plausible animation, or scene dynamics without hand-authoring a physics setup.
