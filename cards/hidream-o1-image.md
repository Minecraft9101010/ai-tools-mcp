## What it does
HiDream-O1 Image generates up to 2048x2048 images in diverse artistic and photorealistic styles with strong text rendering and complex layout handling. It operates directly on raw pixels without a VAE, has built-in reasoning for multi-panel infographics and posters, and supports multiple reference images in a single prompt. Two variants: base (50 steps, higher quality) and dev (28 steps, faster). Ranked #1 open-source image model on the Artificial Analysis leaderboard at time of review.

## When to use it
Generating posters, infographics, or any image that needs accurate text rendering and multiple coherent elements. Inserting specific faces or objects from reference photos into a scene. When you need open-source control over image generation without relying on closed models.
