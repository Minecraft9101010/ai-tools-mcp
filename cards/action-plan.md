## What it does
Plans robot motion sequences by looking ahead at what future frames need to look like while generating current motion, enabling smoother and more coherent actions. Runs up to 9x faster than prior approaches. Demoed on Unitree G1 humanoid robot responding to text commands (jump, sit, clap, walk backward) in real time.

## When to use it
Robotics research requiring natural language-to-motion control. Not a general-purpose tool — currently targeted at humanoid robot control research.
