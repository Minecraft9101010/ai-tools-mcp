# Mama

Open-source multi-person motion capture that turns an ordinary multi-camera video into accurate 3D human movement — no mocap suits or markers needed.

## What it does
Reconstructs detailed 3D animation (pose, shape, hands, and motion over time) for every person in a scene from ordinary multi-camera footage, including hard cases like hugging, dancing, or wrestling where bodies occlude each other. Trained on a purpose-built synthetic dataset (Mama Sin, 2.5 million crops of extreme poses and multi-person contact). Outperforms competing methods on average across benchmarks.

## When to use it
Markerless multi-person motion capture for animation or game production when you have multi-camera footage but not a professional mocap studio.
