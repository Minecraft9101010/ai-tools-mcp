# ChatGPT Work

New "Work" tab in ChatGPT that runs agents over your connected files and platforms (Slack, GitHub, Google Drive, Google Workspace) for autonomous workflows.

- **URL:** https://chatgpt.com (Work tab)
- **Pricing:** freemium (free users limited to the GPT-5.6 Terra model; paid unlocks Soul/Luna and effort levels)
- **Platform:** web
- **Local-friendly:** no (cloud)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-10

## What it does
Turns ChatGPT from a chat interface into an agent workspace: link files and folders across Slack, GitHub, Google Drive, and Google Workspace, then have an agent carry out workflows on them autonomously. Demoed fetching Q1 2026 earnings reports for three companies and producing a polished comparative slide deck in one 26-minute run.

## When to use it
Creating presentations or spreadsheets, analyzing data, and synthesizing information that lives across your connected work platforms, without exporting anything manually.
