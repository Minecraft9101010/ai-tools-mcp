# Google Maps Ask Maps

AI-powered conversational search feature built into Google Maps.

- **URL:** https://maps.google.com (feature within the app)
- **Pricing:** free (within Google Maps)
- **Platform:** mobile | web
- **Local-friendly:** no — cloud feature
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-03-20

## What it does
Embeds a Gemini-powered chat interface directly in Google Maps. Ask natural language questions ("find cozy aesthetic spots near me") and it returns tailored location recommendations by synthesizing real-time traffic data, billions of reviews, photos, and business info — not just a keyword match on listing names.

## When to use it
Discovering places that match a vibe or specific criteria rather than a keyword. Replaces the "ask a local" use case — useful for travel planning, scouting shoot locations, or finding venues that match a specific aesthetic.
