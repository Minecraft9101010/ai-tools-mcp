# Higgsfield Marketing Studio

AI ad production pipeline that generates complete multi-format ad campaigns from a single product link or image.

- **URL:** https://higgsfield.ai
- **Pricing:** paid (check site for current plans)
- **Platform:** web
- **Local-friendly:** no
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-01

## What it does
Paste a product link or upload an image and the system auto-generates ads across nine formats: UGC-style videos, tutorials, cinematic spots, virtual try-ons, and more. Uses the Seed Dance 2.0 engine for consistent avatar/character identity across all generated videos. Supports uploading your own face or using a generated avatar as a brand ambassador.

## When to use it
E-commerce, TikTok Shop, Amazon FBA, or agency work where you need high volume of ad creatives for A/B testing quickly. Especially useful when you want consistent character identity across a full campaign without a production team.
