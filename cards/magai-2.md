# Magai 2

Massive open-source video generator (114B MoE) with native audio, but needs 8x Nvidia Hopper-class GPUs — open in name only for most people.

## What it does
A 114B-parameter mixture-of-experts video generator (audio baked in) currently limited to 10-second generations, with a refiner component upscaling to 1080p. Technically open-source, but at 228GB and requiring 8x Nvidia Hopper GPUs it's out of reach for essentially all consumer or even prosumer hardware.

## When to use it
Not practical for local use outside of well-resourced labs; mentioned mainly for architecture/technical interest.
