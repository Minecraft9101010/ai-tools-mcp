# Logos (Alibaba Tongyi)

Unified generative model for science: one shared token grammar across proteins, small molecules, materials, antibodies, and reaction systems.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub / Hugging Face)
- **Pricing:** open-source (Apache 2)
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (largest 8B model is ~16GB)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-21

## What it does
"Language of generative objects": tokenizes data from multiple scientific domains and trains one model to understand, predict, and generate across them, the way an LLM does with words. Beats comparable models across ligand design, material generation, protein editing, and antibody design benchmarks. Family of three models from 1B to 8B.

## When to use it
Cross-domain scientific design work: protein or antibody design, binding-site prediction, material generation, all from one locally runnable model. Apache 2 permits commercial use, and even the largest variant fits consumer hardware.
