# Higgsfield Soul 2

Higgsfield's in-house foundation image model built for aesthetics, fashion, and creative direction.

- **URL:** https://higgsfield.ai
- **Pricing:** freemium (free tier on Higgsfield platform)
- **Platform:** web
- **Local-friendly:** no (cloud-only)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-03-03

## What it does
Soul 2 is designed as a creative starting point rather than a general-purpose generator. It comes with 20 curated aesthetic presets (waifu cafe, editorial, street style, old smartphone, etc.) and understands fashion culture, camera mediums, and body language. Intended to feed into Nano Banana Pro for downstream editing.

## When to use it
When you want aesthetically strong base images with specific creative direction before running edits in another tool. Works best as the first step in a Higgsfield pipeline.
