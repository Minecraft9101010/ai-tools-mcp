# Gemini Omni Flash

Google's lightweight video generator/editor: plug in any reference video and edit it with natural-language prompts, free daily on Google Flow.

- **URL:** free to try via Google Flow (link in video description)
- **Pricing:** freemium (2-3 free videos/day on Google Flow, resets daily)
- **Platform:** web
- **Local-friendly:** no (cloud model)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-05

## What it does
Faster, cheaper variant of Gemini Omni for video. Accepts existing videos or other references and performs prompt-driven edits: add/replace/remove objects, restyle, VFX. Quality is a step below full Omni; Google's self-reported benchmarks claim it beats Happy Horse, Kling, and Seedance, but the video calls those comparisons misleading (Seedance scores implausibly low).

## When to use it
Free everyday video generation and natural-language video editing via Google Flow. Treat Google's competitive benchmark claims skeptically; Seedance remains stronger in the reviewer's testing.
