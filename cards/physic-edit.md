# Physic Edit

Physics-aware image editor that predicts and renders what happens next to objects under real-world physical forces.

- **URL:** https://github.com (search Physic Edit)
- **Pricing:** open-source (free; based on Qwen Image Edit 2509 LoRA)
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (open-source LoRA on top of Qwen Image Edit)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-03-05

## What it does
Physic Edit edits images by simulating physical phenomena: inserting a straw into a glass with correct refraction, collapsing a house of cards, stretching clay, banana decay, plant growth, ice forming on puddles, butter melting, condensation on glass, paint peeling, and more. Outperforms GPT Image 1.5 and Nano Banana Pro on physics accuracy benchmarks.

## When to use it
Any time you need a physically plausible "what happens next" edit to an image. Better than general image editors for material behavior, biological changes, and thermal/fluid effects.
