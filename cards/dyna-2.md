# Dyna 2

World-action model trained on over 1 million hours of first-person human video (folding clothes, cooking, cleaning) that transfers to robots with only a small amount of robot-specific data.

## What it does
Learns how the world changes when humans interact with it from over 1 million hours of first-person human video (roughly 170 years of continuous experience) — folding clothes, cooking, cleaning, assembling objects. Researchers found a scaling law: more human-video training data improves performance on unseen robot data, meaning the model adapts to different robots with only a small amount of robot-specific fine-tuning, avoiding the usual need for massive robot-specific datasets.

## When to use it
Relevant to robotics researchers interested in transferring human-video-scale pretraining to robot control rather than collecting robot data from scratch.
