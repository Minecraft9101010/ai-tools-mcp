## What it does
Codex watches a screen recording of your workflow (e.g., a full video-upload process with metadata, thumbnail, and captions), extracts where data lives and what the steps are, and saves it as a skill. In a fresh thread it then executes the same workflow on new inputs and verifies the result. Works best on stable workflows with clear success criteria.

## When to use it
Automating repetitive GUI workflows that are hard to describe in text: expense filing, publishing pipelines, bookings. Demonstration-based training instead of prompt trial-and-error. Windows support not yet available.
