# Gemini 3.5 Transcribe

Google's latest transcription model: verbatim or auto-cleaned "smart" transcripts, speaker detection, word timestamps, and seamless multilingual switching.

- **URL:** via AI Studio, Antigravity, Gemini app for macOS, Rambler on Android; Chrome coming soon
- **Pricing:** unknown (available across Google's free and enterprise surfaces)
- **Platform:** web | API | desktop app | mobile
- **Local-friendly:** no (cloud model)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-30

## What it does
Two modes: verbatim (everything exactly as spoken) and smart transcription (strips errors and filler, formats and cleans the text — it can even resolve a stream of self-corrections into the final intent). Handles multiple speakers, word-level timestamps, alphanumerics, and mid-sentence language switching. A live streaming version delivers sub-second latency, and it powers voice control in the Gemini Mac app.

## When to use it
Real-time captions and interactive voice agents (the live version), meeting notes where you want cleaned output rather than raw speech, and multilingual audio that trips up single-language transcribers.
