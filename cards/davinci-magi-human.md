# DaVinci Magi Human

Open-source 15B parameter video generator that produces video with natively integrated audio, including speech and sound effects.

- **URL:** not mentioned (Hugging Face)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** limited — distilled model is 61 GB; not runnable on typical consumer hardware without quantized versions
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-04-01

## What it does
Unified video generation model that creates video and audio together rather than as separate steps. Supports multiple languages. Claims higher win rate than LTX 2.3 in human preference tests (60% preference), with lower error rate on visual quality and text alignment benchmarks. Uncensored out of the box.

## When to use it
Creating short video clips where audio/visual sync is critical — product demos, short films, social content. Most useful once quantized versions become available for consumer hardware.
