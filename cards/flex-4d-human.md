# Flex 4D Human

Reconstructs a full-body moving 3D human (4D: 3D over time) from one or more ordinary videos, viewable from any angle.

## What it does
Takes raw camera footage of a person, no skeletons, depth maps, or surface normals, and reconstructs a moving 3D human model. One video works; adding two or four reference videos makes the reconstruction progressively more accurate and consistent. The resulting 3D model can be dropped into any scene or edited further.

## When to use it
Turning video of a real person into an animatable 3D asset for scenes, VFX, or avatar work, once the code ships. Watch the project page for the release.
