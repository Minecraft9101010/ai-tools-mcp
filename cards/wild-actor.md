## What it does
Generates consistent video of any character — real or cartoon — from 3+ reference images and a text prompt. Maintains face, outfit, and appearance across the entire generated clip. Works with both photorealistic subjects and animated characters.

## When to use it
Generating consistent character footage for trailers, social content, or story visualization when you have reference images but no filmed material. Good for cartoon/IP character video generation without rigging or 3D workflows.
