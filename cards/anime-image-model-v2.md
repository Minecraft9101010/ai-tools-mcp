# Anime Image Model v2 Preview

Compact state-of-the-art anime image generator with Danbooru tag support.

- **URL:** not mentioned (HuggingFace)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes — ~4.18GB (2B parameters); fits on most consumer GPUs; faster than models like "Z image"
- **Availability:** available (v2 preview)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-03-20

## What it does
Generates anime, manga, and stylized illustration images from text prompts. Supports both Danbooru-style tag prompts (one girl, character names, artist tags, quality scores) and natural language. Can produce rough line art sketches, multiple anime styles, and existing character renditions. Trained on several million anime images and ~800k non-anime artistic images — no synthetic/AI-generated training data. Knowledge cutoff September 2025. Not suited for photorealism.

## When to use it
Anime fan art, manga-style illustration generation, stylized character design. Tag-based prompting makes it highly controllable for users familiar with Danbooru. Small model size makes it practical on consumer hardware.
