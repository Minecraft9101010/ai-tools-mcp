## What it does
UniGeo reconstructs the 3D point cloud of an input image and then re-renders it from a precisely specified camera position. You can specify pan and tilt in degrees, chain multiple movements (pan left 16°, then tilt up 7°), and the model accurately repositions the viewpoint. Unlike standard image editors (Ideogram, GPT Image) where you describe movement in prose and get approximate results, UniGeo gives exact degree-level control.

## When to use it
Product photography where you need the same shot from slightly different camera angles. Architecture or real estate images where precise viewpoint control matters. Any workflow where the imprecision of natural-language image editing is a problem.
