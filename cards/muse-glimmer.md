# Muse Glimmer (Meta)

Meta's 30B dense open-source model, positioned against Gemma 4 and Qwen 3.6 — but independent leaderboard results don't support Meta's own comparison chart.

- **URL:** not mentioned (HuggingFace)
- **Pricing:** open-source (Apache 2.0)
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (~60GB full; smaller GGUF quantizations available)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-16

## What it does
A 30B-parameter dense (not MoE) model. Meta's own release page compares it favorably to Gemma 4 and Qwen 3.6, but highlights the best score in black rather than the usual blue in a way the source video calls misleading — and on the independent Artificial Analysis leaderboard, Muse Glimmer does not outperform Qwen 3.6, despite Qwen 3.6 having fewer parameters (with Qwen 3.8 already superseding it).

## When to use it
Worth downloading only if you specifically want a Meta-trained dense (non-MoE) model; Qwen 3.6/3.8 are rated better by independent benchmarks at this size.
