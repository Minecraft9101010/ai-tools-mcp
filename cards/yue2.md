# YuE2

YuE2 is an open-source AI music generator for creating, editing, and covering songs from text prompts or reference audio.

- **URL:** https://github.com/multimodal-art-projection/YuE
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes, the video shows it running locally in ComfyUI and says it can run with 4 GB of VRAM or less using a quantized model.
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-15

## What it does
YuE2 generates full songs from style prompts and lyrics, including multilingual vocals and instrumental-only tracks. It can also use reference audio to preserve melody or structure while changing style, lyrics, key, or arrangement.

## When to use it
Use it when you want a local/open-source AI music generator for text-to-music, cover songs, style transfer, or iterative song editing.
