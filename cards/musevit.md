# MuseViT

Vision model that reads and understands sheet music, trained on 9.7M pages via masked reconstruction.

- **URL:** not mentioned (model released, linked in video description)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (model is only a few hundred MB)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-05

## What it does
Understands the full structure of sheet music (symbols, timing, pitch, staff lines, spacing) rather than treating a score as a flat image, and it clearly beats general vision models on sheet music recognition and classification benchmarks. Trained on ~9.7M pages covering ~400K works by masking sections and learning to reconstruct them.

## When to use it
Digitizing or analyzing sheet music, building music OCR pipelines, or any task where a general vision model fails on musical notation. Small enough to run on most consumer devices.
