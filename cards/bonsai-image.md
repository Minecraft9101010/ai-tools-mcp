# Bonsai Image

Heavily compressed Flux 2 Klein image generator that runs fully offline on a phone.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** mobile | local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (~1GB, runs on an iPhone)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-31

## What it does
Not a new model architecture — it's Flux 2 Klein (normally ~8GB) compressed down to roughly 1GB, released in a 1-bit variant and a ternary (3-value weight) variant with slightly better quality. Runs locally and offline on an iPhone or similar consumer device; demoed generating a 512x512 image in 9.4 seconds on an iPhone 17 Pro Max.

## When to use it
On-device, offline image generation on a phone with no cloud dependency, when Flux-2-Klein-level quality at a fraction of the size is enough.
