# OmniShotCut

Tiny AI model (164 MB) that detects exact timestamps and transition types (hard cut, dissolve, fade, slide, cross zoom) in any video.

- **URL:** not mentioned (HuggingFace space + GitHub)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local | web (HuggingFace Space)
- **Local-friendly:** yes (164 MB; very lightweight)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-03

## What it does
OmniShotCut analyzes a video and outputs the timestamp and transition type for every cut in the video. Trained on 2.5M raw internet videos and 300K synthetic training videos with 11M+ labeled transitions. Handles: hard cuts, dissolves, fades, slides, cross zooms, and more. Useful for automated video analysis, editing automation, or building cut detection into a pipeline.

## When to use it
Automating the detection of edit points in long-form video for downstream processing. Building video analysis pipelines where you need to segment by scene. Useful for content aggregators, highlight reel generators, or anyone processing large libraries of video footage.
