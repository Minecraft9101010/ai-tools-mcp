## What it does
A simple agentic framework wrapped around a frozen model that lets it learn and apply new patterns on the fly in ARC-AGI-3's unseen video-game environments — 100 across all 25 environments, all 183 levels (evaluated on the public set, so the score may be inflated).

## When to use it
Less a product than proof of a principle: you can unlock large capability gains by optimizing the harness around a model instead of the model itself. Worth reading if you build agent scaffolding.
