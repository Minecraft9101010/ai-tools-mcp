# Happy Horse (Alibaba)

Alibaba's video generation model, ranked #1 on Artificial Analysis at launch but underperforms in real-world testing relative to its leaderboard position.

## What it does
Happy Horse is Alibaba's latest text-to-video and image-to-video model. Ranked #1 on the Artificial Analysis leaderboard for both categories at launch, with a substantial lead over Kling 2.0. However, in the reviewer's tests, physics were inconsistent, prompt adherence was poor, and results fell clearly short of Kling 2.0 on complex prompts (dragon chase scene, satellite-to-street zoom). The reviewer did not produce a full review video.

## When to use it
Low-stakes or experimental video generation where you want to use free credits to evaluate yourself. Not the current best choice for complex, physics-dependent, or prompt-faithful video generation based on independent testing.
