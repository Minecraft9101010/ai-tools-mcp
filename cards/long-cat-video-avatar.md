# Long Cat Video Avatar 1.5 (Meituan)

Open-source talking avatar generator that creates lip-synced video from a reference image and audio input.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** no (16GB VRAM for int8)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-24

## What it does
Takes a reference image and audio track and generates a realistic talking-head video with accurate lip sync. Supports multiple visual styles and multi-person scenes. Int8 quantized version requires 16GB VRAM.

## When to use it
Talking head video production, dubbing and localization, virtual spokespersons, or e-commerce presenters where you need a human face delivering audio content without filming.
