## What it does
Gemini 3.8 Live supports real-time voice conversation, live visual inputs, multilingual switching, and background tool/API calls while continuing to speak. The video also mentions an Extended Thinking variant for deeper multi-step reasoning.

## When to use it
Use it for real-time voice agents, multimodal assistants, camera-based help, and low-cost live AI voice applications.
