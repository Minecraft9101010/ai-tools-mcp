# Crisper Whisper 2

## What it does
Transcribes audio in two modes — verbatim (keeps stutters, hesitations, laughter, meta tags, repeats) or intended (a cleaned, polished transcript) — plus precise word-level start/end timestamps, across many languages. On the team's own benchmark it beats 11Labs and other leading transcription tools on both accuracy and word-timestamp precision. Released in four sizes (0.2B up to 2B parameters).

## When to use it
Transcription needing accurate word-level timing (captioning, dubbing sync) or a choice between raw verbatim and cleaned-up output, as a free local alternative to cloud transcription APIs.
