## What it does
Given a question like disease-risk or food-shortage mapping, it automatically finds relevant geographic data, cleans and combines it, trains ML models, and evaluates predictions — compressing weeks of specialist work into minutes. Results: Nigeria food security accuracy 31% to 66%; DRC Ebola outbreak prediction 88%, over 10 points above prior state of the art.
