# Abot World

Never-ending interactive world generator that streams 720p at 16 fps in real time on a single consumer GPU.

- **URL:** not mentioned (code + Hugging Face model released)
- **Pricing:** open-source (Apache 2.0, commercial use allowed)
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (24GB total; runs on an RTX 5090, potentially a 4090)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-07-12

## What it does
Generates infinite explorable worlds you control in real time, with a bit over 1 second of latency and sessions that can run for over an hour. Its differentiator: other world generators sustain only seconds to minutes of interactive video, while this one runs essentially forever, across many environments and characters.

## When to use it
Experimenting with playable generated worlds locally, or any project needing long-running interactive world video on consumer hardware with a permissive commercial license.
