Official training and fine-tuning package for the LTX video model: train LoRAs for consistent characters, objects, styles, or VFX.

## What it does
Fine-tunes LTX (whose 2.3 release is the leading open video model with native audio) on your own data. Supports a wide range of workflows: video extension, inpainting and outpainting, text-to-audio, audio extension/inpainting, and video-to-video transformations. Ships with a quick-start guide and dataset preparation instructions.

## When to use it
When base LTX can't reliably produce your specific character, camera effect, or transformation style: train a LoRA on it instead of prompt-wrangling. The official route for customizing the LTX ecosystem.
