# Retime GS

Research model that converts regular 2D video footage of people into smooth, viewable 4D (3D + time) animations.

## What it does
Takes monocular 2D video as input and outputs a 4D scene — a 3D reconstruction that can be viewed from any angle over time. Handles choppy or sparse video by interpolating missing frames using continuous-time 3D representations, motion tracking, optical flow, and improved 4D Gaussian splatting. Outperforms competing 4D reconstruction methods like Deform, Gaussian Flow, and 2D Lift.

## When to use it
Creating 3D character animations from regular video without specialized capture equipment. Useful for VFX, gaming asset creation, or research into human motion reconstruction.
