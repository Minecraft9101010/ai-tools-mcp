## What it does
Anthropic reports Opus 4.8 is more likely to flag uncertainty and less likely to make unsupported claims than prior Claude models, and is 4x less likely to let flaws in generated code go unnoticed. It also pushes back on weak plans during agentic workflows. Independent results are mixed: Artificial Analysis ranks Opus 4.8 Max #1 overall but only one point above GPT-5.5, with open-source Kimi K2.6 close behind; GPT-5.5 still leads agentic terminal coding and the Omniscience accuracy index (where Gemini 3.1 Pro and Gemini Flash also beat Opus 4.8); LiveBench (Abacus AI) still ranks GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3.1 Pro above it. Hallucination rate is unchanged from 4.7.

## When to use it
Agentic coding and long-running autonomous workflows where reduced hallucination and self-flagged uncertainty matter more than topping every leaderboard. Not a clear win over GPT-5.5 on raw accuracy or terminal coding — check the specific benchmark that matches your use case before switching.
