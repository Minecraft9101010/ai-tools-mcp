## What it does
AlphaEvolve generates candidate algorithms, tests them, and evolves better versions over iterations. Documented achievements: 30% reduction in DNA sequencing errors (Google DeepConsensus), improved electricity grid feasibility from 14% to 88%, 5% improvement in Google Earth AI disaster prediction, 10x lower error rates in quantum circuits for Google's Willow processor, and assisted in designing next-gen TPUs. Effectively an automated algorithm invention engine.
