# Magenta Real-Time 2 (Google)

Open-source real-time music generator that responds to live MIDI, audio, and text input in roughly 200ms, designed to behave like a playable instrument rather than a prompt-to-file generator.

- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-07

## What it does
Generates live music from MIDI notes, a style prompt, or an audio reference, responding to what you play as you play it. Cuts response latency from the first version's roughly 3 seconds down to about 200ms, and supports duplex communication (listening while generating). Designed to plug into DAWs as a live instrument rather than a batch text-to-song tool.

## When to use it
Live music production or jamming where you want an AI that responds in real time to MIDI/audio input, not a tool that renders a finished track from a text prompt. Runs on a laptop, no GPU needed.
