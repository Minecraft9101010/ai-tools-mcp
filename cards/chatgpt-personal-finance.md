# ChatGPT Personal Finance

ChatGPT feature that connects to your financial accounts via Plaid and answers questions based on actual data.

- **URL:** not mentioned
- **Pricing:** paid (Pro plan)
- **Platform:** web | mobile
- **Local-friendly:** no
- **Availability:** limited (Pro subscribers, US only, preview)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-17

## What it does
Connects ChatGPT to bank accounts, credit cards, and investments through Plaid. Shows a spending/subscriptions/investments dashboard and lets you ask AI questions grounded in your actual financial data. Read-only access.

## When to use it
Answering "where am I overspending?" or similar questions from an AI that sees your real accounts rather than hypothetical scenarios.
