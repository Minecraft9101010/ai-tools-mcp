## What it does
One model covering the whole 3D pipeline: text-to-3D generation, prompt-based editing of existing models ("turn the head into a bull's head", "remove the sail", "add glasses"), and part segmentation that splits any 3D model into separate components. Most 3D AI tools do only one of these.

## When to use it
Watch for the release if you edit or kitbash 3D assets — prompt-driven edits and automatic part separation on existing models is the differentiator over single-purpose 3D generators.
