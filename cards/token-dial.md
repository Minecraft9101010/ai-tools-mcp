## What it does
Lets you control specific properties of video generation — things like explosion smokiness, fire color, aurora brightness, subject age, or dancing intensity — with continuous sliders instead of relying on imprecise prompt language. Gives fine-grained, quantifiable control over video edits.

## When to use it
When you need precise iterative control over video attributes rather than guessing how to phrase a prompt. Useful for visual effects work where subtle adjustments matter.
