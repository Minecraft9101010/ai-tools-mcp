A biological foundation model trained on 9 trillion DNA base pairs that can understand, classify, and generate DNA sequences across all domains of life.

## What it does
Treats DNA like a language and applies LLM-style training on 9 trillion base pairs from the Open Genome 2 dataset (bacteria, plants, fungi, animals, humans). Has a 1-million-token context window with single-nucleotide resolution — sufficient to model entire chromosomes and long-range regulatory interactions. Can detect patterns linked to disease, predict effects of genetic mutations, and generate complete synthetic DNA sequences for novel organisms. Published in Nature.

## When to use it
Research applications in genomics, drug discovery, synthetic biology, or precision medicine. No public consumer access; relevant primarily as a signal of where biological AI is heading.
