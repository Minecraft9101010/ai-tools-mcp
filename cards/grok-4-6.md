## What it does
Matches GPT-5.6 Soul Max and Claude Fable 5 on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index, beats them both on GDPval (real professional-job performance), though it lags GPT-5.6 specifically on Deep SWE (agentic software engineering). Trained on a wide range of agentic RL tasks; notably strong at turning a vague idea into a working first version and doing more self-checking mid-task. Faster than GPT or Claude (though not as fast as DeepSeek V4 Pro) at a cost similar to Qwen 3.8 Max — cheaper than GPT-5.6.

## When to use it
A cost-efficient frontier-tier pick for agentic coding and general reasoning when you want near-GPT-5.6-level intelligence without GPT/Claude pricing. Available in Cursor and Grok Build as well as via API.
