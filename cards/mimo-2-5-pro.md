## What it does
MiMo 2.5 Pro is Xiaomi's strongest model, matching Kimi K 2.6 on agentic coding benchmarks and tying for the #1 open-source position on Artificial Analysis. Demonstrated coding feat: built a full-featured desktop video editor (multiple tracks, clip trimming, cross fades, audio mixing, export) — 8,000+ lines of code, 1,800 tool calls, 11.5 hours of autonomous work. Text-only but designed for deep agentic workflows with diverse tool use. Also released: MiMo 2.5 (multimodal variant with image and audio understanding).

## When to use it
Long autonomous agentic coding tasks. Comparing against Kimi K 2.6 for your specific use case. Once open-sourced, a strong candidate for local deployment of a frontier-tier model.
