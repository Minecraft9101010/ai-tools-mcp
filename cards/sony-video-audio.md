# Sony MMHNet (Video-to-Audio)

## What it does
Generates audio that syncs tightly to on-screen action in videos up to 5 minutes. Uses a multi-mobile hierarchical network (MMHNet) with Mamba architecture for long-form coherence. Compared favorably to MM Audio in sync accuracy, especially for impact sounds. Not suitable for speech generation (outputs non-verbal audio only).

## When to use it
Adding realistic sound effects to video without speech. Best when audio-visual sync is important and the scene has physical actions or environmental sounds. Not a replacement for a voice/music tool.
