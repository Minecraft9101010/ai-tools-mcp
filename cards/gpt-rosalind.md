# GPT Rosalind

OpenAI reasoning model designed specifically for life sciences research, with access to 50+ scientific databases.

- **URL:** https://openai.com (request access page)

## What it does
Reasoning model optimized for drug discovery, genomics, and protein engineering workflows. Connects to 50+ scientific databases via a life sciences Codex plugin, enabling literature review, experimental planning, and data analysis in a single workflow. Outperforms GPT-5.4 across scientific benchmarks including experimental design and analysis.

## When to use it
Life sciences researchers who need to move from raw data to hypotheses and experimental plans. Bridges literature search, protein databases, and sequence databases without switching tools.
