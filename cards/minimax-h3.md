# MiniMax H3

MiniMax's flagship multimodal video generator (successor to the HighLaw/Hailuo line) — 2K video from text, image, video, and audio references, with open-sourcing planned.

- **URL:** not mentioned (MiniMax platform)
- **Pricing:** paid (roughly $1.20 per 10-second clip on MiniMax's own platform at review time; open weights promised)
- **Platform:** web | API
- **Local-friendly:** no (closed, cloud-only until the open-source release)
- **Availability:** limited (available on MiniMax's platform now; open-weight release announced but not yet shipped)
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-02

## What it does
Generates 2K video from text, image, video, and audio references — trailers, ads built from a storyboard plus logo, or full music videos from a song, image, and style reference. Roughly 3x cheaper per second than ByteDance's SeeDance 2.5 on MiniMax's own platform. MiniMax announced it will open-source the model.

## When to use it
Reference-heavy video generation (trailers, ads, music videos) at a meaningfully lower price than SeeDance 2.5, while it's still cloud-only; worth rechecking once the open-source release lands. See also H3Max, Stability AI's separate paid speed-optimized fine-tune of this model.
