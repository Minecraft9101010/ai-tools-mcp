# Anima v3 (Anime Image Model)

Lightweight 2B parameter model for high-quality anime and non-photorealistic art generation, the fastest and best in its class as of April 2025.

- **URL:** https://civitai.com (also ModelScope)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local (ComfyUI)
- **Local-friendly:** yes (2B parameters, very fast)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-01

## What it does
Image generation model trained on millions of anime images and 800K non-anime art images. Understands Danbooru tags and Stable Diffusion quality tags. Works smoothly in ComfyUI.

## When to use it
Generating anime-style images or non-photorealistic art locally with minimal VRAM and fast generation times.
