# L2P

Open-source pixel-space image diffusion model with 4K/8K resolution extrapolation and no latent space.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** no (mid-to-high GPU required, ~20GB model)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-24

## What it does
Generates images directly in pixel space rather than a compressed latent space, eliminating latent-space artifacts. Currently generates 1K images with the ability to extrapolate up to 4K/8K. Model weight is approximately 20GB.

## When to use it
High-fidelity image generation where latent compression artifacts are a problem, or when fine-tuning custom styles directly in pixel space matters more than speed.
