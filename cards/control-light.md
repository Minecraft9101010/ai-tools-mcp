# Control Light

Open-source generative tool for fixing dark images or adjusting lighting brightness without the artifacts of a manual exposure slider.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (built on Flux 2 Klein, runs on consumer devices)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-31

## What it does
Generatively brightens images by understanding how real photos should look, rather than just cranking exposure. Learns a continuous range of brightness levels (not just single before/after pairs) and keeps image structure stable as light changes, avoiding the noise and artifacts a traditional brightness slider introduces. Built on Flux 2 Klein. Code, weights, training scripts, and training dataset are all released.

## When to use it
Restoring underexposed photos, night scenes, or surveillance footage where pushing a standard brightness slider introduces too much noise and washes out detail.
