# MocapAnything v2

End-to-end motion capture model that converts any video (humans, animals, fictional characters) directly into animation-ready joint rotations without the artifacts of older two-step systems.

## What it does
MocapAnything v2 takes a video of any moving subject and outputs clean joint rotation data suitable for 3D character animation. Unlike previous systems that split pose estimation and inverse kinematics into separate steps (causing jitter and unnatural spinning), v2 learns pose + joint rotations end-to-end in one unified model, self-correcting toward animation quality. Works across wildly different skeleton types: humans, animals, fictional characters with non-human limbs.

## When to use it
Animating 3D characters from reference video without a motion capture suit. VFX, game development, robotics (extracting motion priors from video). Replacing manual rigging for non-humanoid characters where standard Mocap fails.
