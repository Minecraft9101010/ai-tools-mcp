# Gemini App (updated)

Google's AI assistant, upgraded to a proactive daily assistant with background agents.

- **URL:** gemini.google.com (iOS/Android)
- **Pricing:** freemium (Plus subscribers for Daily Brief, some features Pro/Ultra only)
- **Platform:** web | mobile
- **Local-friendly:** no (cloud-based)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-21

## What it does
Delivers a Daily Brief summarizing Gmail and Calendar, runs background agents that monitor the web 24/7, and handles agentic booking inside Google Search. Surfaces relevant info before you ask for it.

## When to use it
Morning briefings from inbox and calendar, background monitoring for apartments or flights, or having Google call businesses on your behalf.
