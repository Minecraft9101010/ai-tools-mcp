# Index TTS 2.5

Open-source voice cloning TTS that clones a voice from seconds of audio, handles emotion and multiple languages, and can dub a video into another language in the cloned voice.

- **URL:** not mentioned (HuggingFace)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (~5.5GB total)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-16

## What it does
Clones a reference voice from a short sample and generates new speech — including angry/emotional delivery and multiple languages (demoed with Spanish) — while sounding close to the original voice. Can dub existing video/scene audio into another language in the same cloned voice. Successor to the team's earlier version 2 model.

## When to use it
Local voice cloning, multilingual dubbing in a consistent voice, or emotional TTS on consumer hardware.
