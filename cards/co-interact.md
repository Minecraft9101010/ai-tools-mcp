# Co-Interact

AI that generates realistic UGC/influencer-style product introduction videos from just a product photo, a person photo, and a text prompt.

## What it does
Co-Interact takes two images (product photo + person photo) and a prompt with step-by-step instructions, and generates a video of that person naturally introducing and handling the product. Uses dual-stream code generation: one stream handles pixels, another handles the physical relationship between the human and the object to ensure interactions are physically plausible. Supports multi-step sequential instructions in the prompt.

## When to use it
E-commerce product videos without hiring talent or filming. Generating UGC-style content at scale for ads or social media. Mass-producing product introduction content with different people and prompts.
