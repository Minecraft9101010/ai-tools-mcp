# GenRecon

Turns casual smartphone video or photos of a room into a full editable 3D scene with materials, for real estate/VR use.

## What it does
Takes a set of images or a video walkthrough of a room and outputs a PBR-ready mesh (a 3D model with materials suitable for rendering/relighting). Generates the scene in consistent chunks and uses Trellis 2 as a generative shape prior, giving the model a strong sense of realistic 3D shape beyond only what's directly visible in the source images. Once generated, the scene can be relit from different angles or have objects/surfaces edited.

## When to use it
Real estate virtual tours or interior design visualization once released — turning a phone walkthrough into an editable, relightable 3D scene rather than a static tour.
