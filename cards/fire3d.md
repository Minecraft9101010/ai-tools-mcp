# Fire3D

An AI system for converting photos or videos into simulation-ready editable 3D scenes.

- **URL:** https://xiahongchi.github.io/Fire3D/
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes, the transcript says code, training code, and local run instructions are released
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-13

## What it does
Fire3D reconstructs complete 3D scenes with separate mesh objects and material information from images or video. The video says it can process scenes quickly and handle up to 16 objects in parallel on one GPU.

## When to use it
Use it for fast 3D scene reconstruction, editable mesh generation, and robotics or simulation-ready scene assets.
