# Soul World Model

An AI video world model that generates navigable, realistic street-level tours of real cities — currently trained on Seoul, Korea.

## What it does
Uses RAG on millions of street-view images to generate coherent long-form video you can navigate freely (walk anywhere in the city, turn directions, enter parks). Supports text-prompted effects — you can add a wave flooding the street, change time of day, or summon a UFO. Unlike standard video generators, it maintains quality over multi-kilometer trajectories without accumulating errors by using look-ahead scene planning and cross-temporal interpolation.

## When to use it
Virtual city exploration, autonomous driving simulation datasets, digital twin prototyping, or novel real-time interactive video experiences. Currently Seoul-only; intended to expand to other cities.
