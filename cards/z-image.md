# Z Image

Previous best open-source image generator (ranked #2 after Ernie Image as of April 2025), known for better anatomy handling.

- **URL:** https://huggingface.co (search Z Image)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local (ComfyUI)
- **Local-friendly:** yes
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-01

## What it does
Open-source image generator with strong photorealism and anatomy handling. Beats Ernie Image on complex physics, anatomy prompts, and spatial understanding tasks. Weaker than Ernie Image on text rendering, detailed prompt adherence, artistic styles, and infographics.

## When to use it
When anatomy accuracy or physics-based prompts are the priority over text rendering and compositional detail. Falls back below Ernie Image in most other categories.
