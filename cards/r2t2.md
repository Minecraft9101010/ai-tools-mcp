An open-source real-time speech transcription model.

- **URL:** https://r2t2.ai/
- **Pricing:** open-source

## What it does
R2T2 turns live speech into text in real time. The video highlights low word error rate, low latency, and support for English and Chinese benchmarks.

## When to use it
Use it for local live transcription where latency and accuracy matter.
