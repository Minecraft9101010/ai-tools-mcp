# Vista 4D

Converts a regular video into a full 4D scene (3D + time), enabling arbitrary camera repositioning, object insertion/removal, and scene extrapolation.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub released)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (~21 GB; fits on mid/high-end consumer GPUs)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-03

## What it does
Vista 4D reconstructs a 3D point cloud from a normal video, then lets you: reshoot the scene from any camera angle, insert new objects or entire videos into the scene as 3D point clouds, remove objects from the scene, extrapolate what exists outside the frame, and merge multiple video scenes. Outperforms RecamMaster and Trajectory Crafter on coherence and detail preservation. Model is ~21 GB.

## When to use it
Film or video post-production where you need to change camera angles after the fact. Adding or removing elements from existing footage. Creating parallax effects or camera fly-throughs from static or fixed-angle source video. Merging footage from different shooting locations into one coherent scene.
