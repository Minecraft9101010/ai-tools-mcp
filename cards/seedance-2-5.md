# Seedance 2.5 (on Higgsfield)

Cloud video generator on the Higgsfield platform: up to 30-second multi-shot narrative videos with audio, driven by up to 50 references.

- **URL:** https://higgsfield.ai
- **Pricing:** paid (Higgsfield platform; promo periods vary)
- **Platform:** web
- **Local-friendly:** no (cloud-only)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-15

## What it does
Generates up to 30 seconds of video in a single pass with multiple shots, narrative structure, and built-in audio, and can extend existing generations while keeping characters, locations, and pacing consistent. Accepts up to 50 references at once (30 images, 10 videos, 10 audio) covering characters, environment, style, motion, and soundtrack — even a rough 3D clay render as a camera/composition guide. Supports timestamp-level editing: change one section, swap the environment, or move the camera while preserving performances.

## When to use it
Cloud option for multi-shot short-form video (short films, commercials, music videos) where reference-driven consistency and post-hoc section editing matter and running locally isn't a requirement.
