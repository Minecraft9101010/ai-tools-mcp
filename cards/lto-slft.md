# LTO / SLFT (Apple)

Open-source single-image 3D reconstruction with view-dependent reflections and surface detail.

## What it does
Takes a single photo and generates a 3D model with accurate reflections and surface details. Outperforms Trellis on reconstruction quality. Output is view-dependent, meaning lighting and reflections shift correctly as the camera moves.

## When to use it
3D asset creation from photos for game or VR pipelines, product visualization, or any workflow where a single reference image needs to become a usable 3D model.
