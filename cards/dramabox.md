## What it does
TTS model built on LTX 2.3 with voice cloning and director-style stage direction support. Distinctive feature: adds phonetic vocalizations (laughter, sighs, pauses) and inline sound effect tags. Peaks at 24GB VRAM.

## When to use it
Expressive voice acting for games, audiobooks, or video. Choose over Seen Ma Audio when you specifically need phonetic vocalizations or inline SFX tagging.
