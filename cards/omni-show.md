# Omni Show (ByteDance)

ByteDance's AI system for generating realistic UGC/marketing videos of a fictional or cloned person presenting any product.

## What it does
Takes reference images of a person and product plus optional audio input, then generates a realistic video of that person presenting the product. Supports custom audio to control exactly what the person says, and pose skeleton video to control precise movements including hands and fingers. More consistent character/product identity than competitors like Xunyan Custom, Humo, and Vase.

## When to use it
Mass-producing UGC-style product demo videos for influencers or brands without requiring the actual person to record. Also useful for voice-cloned spokesperson content.
