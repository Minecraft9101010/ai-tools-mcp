# Carbon (DNA Foundation Model)

Tiny open-source DNA foundation model for sequence completion, variant scoring, and protein structure prediction.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (smallest variant is 1GB)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-05-24

## What it does
Processes up to ~400K DNA base pairs. Can continue DNA sequences, score genetic variants, and predict protein 3D structures. The 500M parameter (smallest) variant weighs 1GB. 275x faster than EVO 2 at comparable tasks.

## When to use it
Bioinformatics workflows involving sequence completion, variant effect scoring, or connecting genetic sequences to protein structures. The small footprint makes it practical without a cluster.
