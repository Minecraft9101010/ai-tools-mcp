# Muse Spark (Meta)

Meta's new closed-source AI model from their rebuilt research team, powering Meta AI across Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp.

- **URL:** https://meta.ai (embedded in Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Meta smart glasses)
- **Pricing:** free (consumer products)
- **Platform:** web | mobile
- **Local-friendly:** no (closed source)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-06-01

## What it does
Multimodal model (text plus images/figures). Ranked 4th on artificial analysis leaderboard tied with Sonnet 4.6. Particularly strong on scientific figure understanding, math, and agentic research. Underperforms GPT-5.4 and Gemini 3.1 Pro on most other benchmarks. Closed source unlike previous Llama models.

## When to use it
Quick AI assistance within Meta's ecosystem. Not a primary choice if you have access to frontier models.
