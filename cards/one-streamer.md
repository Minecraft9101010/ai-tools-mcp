## What it does
A conversational avatar you talk to in real time — natural facial expression, hand movement, and roughly 200ms model-side response latency at 25fps, all from one transformer model that interleaves text, audio, and video. Supports duplex communication (listens while responding instead of strict turn-taking). Current version is low-resolution (192p) and described as a proof of concept.

Not yet usable. Worth tracking for future real-time conversational avatar products.
