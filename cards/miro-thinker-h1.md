## What it does
A reasoning-capable open-source model (part of the Miro Thinker 1.7 / H1 family) that excels at deep research, frontier science tasks, and financial analysis — benchmarked above GPT-5.4, Gemini 3.1 Pro, and Claude Opus 4.6 on several research tasks. Notably accurate at prediction: when given a gold price forecast task, it was off by only $4 (0.08%) over a 15-day window.

## When to use it
Deep research queries, literature synthesis, financial forecasting, or any task where thoroughness and multi-step reasoning matter more than speed. Could be worth testing for prediction market applications.
