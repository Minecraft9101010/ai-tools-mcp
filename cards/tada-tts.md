## What it does
Voice cloning and TTS generator — upload a reference voice sample and it synthesizes new speech in that voice from a transcript. Described as "insanely" natural-sounding. Fast inference. Compared favorably to Fish Audio S2 in the same video.

## When to use it
Dubbing, voiceover for content where you have a reference voice, or generating speech that needs to sound like a specific person. Good for social video voiceover, podcast production, or AI-assisted content localization.
