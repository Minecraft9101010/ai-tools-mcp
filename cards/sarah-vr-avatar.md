## What it does
Sarah generates a full-body animated character visible in VR that understands speech, responds in real time, generates dynamic gestures and body movement (not just lip-sync), tracks the user's head position for natural eye contact, and includes configurable gaze intensity. Runs fast enough for live VR sessions.

## When to use it
Interactive VR companion or training scenarios where you want a lifelike responsive character rather than a static avatar. Currently research-stage with no public model release.
