# DreamVerse

A near-real-time AI video generation and editing platform built on top of Fast Video (LTX 2.3), capable of generating and editing 5-second 1080p clips in seconds.

- **URL:** not mentioned (demo link referenced in video description)
- **Pricing:** unknown
- **Platform:** web
- **Local-friendly:** no (runs on enterprise-grade B200 GPU infrastructure)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-03-27

## What it does
Enter a text prompt and get a 5-second 1080p video within a few seconds (claimed 4.5 seconds on B200 GPU). You can then edit iteratively: change characters, swap styles (e.g. "anime"), all in ~3 seconds. Built on Fast Video / LTX 2.3, which has 10x+ lower latency than standard LTX 2.3 for both text-to-video and image-to-video tasks.

## When to use it
Rapid video prototyping, iterative style exploration, or any workflow where near-real-time video generation feedback matters. Not yet photorealistic — has edge distortions — but the iteration speed is the differentiator.
