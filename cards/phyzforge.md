## What it does
PhyzForge generates 3D assets that are physically grounded, not just visually appealing. Stage 1: a vision-language model acts as a physical architect, generating a blueprint (parts, bounding boxes, joint types, materials, mass, relationships). Stage 2: a diffusion model turns that blueprint into a high-quality 3D asset with geometry, texture, and kinematic parameters. The result can be correctly manipulated by robotic arms and accurately simulated in physics engines.

## When to use it
Creating 3D assets for robotics training environments, game simulations, or any application where objects need to behave physically correctly (not just look right). Better than standard 3D generators when you need joints, physics constraints, and interaction logic.
