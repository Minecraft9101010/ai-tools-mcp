# SolarWM

SolarWM is an open-source framework for converting video models into long-running real-time interactive worlds.

- **URL:** https://junchao-cs.github.io/SolarWM-Web/?utm_source=chatgpt.com
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local, web
- **Local-friendly:** yes, the transcript says the project, models, data pipeline, and training recipes are open-sourced for download.
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-09-06

## What it does
It works with multiple video generators, including Minimax H3, and can maintain consistent generated worlds for over an hour. The project also releases datasets, checkpoints, and training components.

## When to use it
Use it to train or study interactive world models, or to adapt video generators into controllable virtual environments.
