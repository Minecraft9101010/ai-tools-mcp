# Spark VSR

Google's open-source video super-resolution model, described as the best open-source video upscaler available.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub repo linked in video description)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes (requires higher-end GPU; model weights 42.2 GB)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-03-27

## What it does
Takes low-quality video and outputs a clean high-resolution version. Works across wildlife footage, scenery, old movies, 3D animation, and artistic styles. Full training code, inference code, and dataset were released alongside the model.

## When to use it
Upscaling archival or low-res footage, restoring old movies, or improving output quality from video generation pipelines — when you have GPU resources and want the current open-source quality ceiling.
