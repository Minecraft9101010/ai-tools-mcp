# WanGP (Wan2GP)

Low-VRAM platform for running open-source video generators, including MiniMax H3 on as little as 5 GB of VRAM.

- **URL:** not mentioned (GitHub)
- **Pricing:** open-source
- **Platform:** local
- **Local-friendly:** yes — built specifically for low-VRAM GPUs (5-6 GB)
- **Availability:** available
- **Last reviewed:** 2026-08-05

## What it does
An alternative to ComfyUI aimed at "GPU poor" hardware. Added MiniMax H3 support within days of release, running it at 480p on 5 GB of VRAM through aggressive optimization.

## When to use it
Running current open video models on GPUs below the ~12 GB floor that even pruned ComfyUI workflows need. The escape hatch when your card can't fit the standard quantized models.
